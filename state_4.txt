## State: **Reach Object**

---

## 🎯 GOAL
Move precisely toward the desired object until you are close enough to stop and describe it.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES

- Advance gradually toward the object using small forward steps (`A1`).
- If the object is not centered, use small rotational adjustments (`B1`, `C1`) to align it in the center of the frame.
- Use lateral movements (`D1` or `D2`) **only** if the object is close and slightly off-center.
- Consider the object “close enough” if:
  - It fills at least 70% of the image width,
  - The top and bottom edges of the object are not visible,
  - And it appears centered (within ±10% from the horizontal center).
- If any of these conditions are not met, continue approaching or adjusting alignment.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return **ONE** movement command from the list below:

### A. Forward movement:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm

### B. Left rotation:
- `B1`: rotate 15°

### C. Right rotation:
- `C1`: rotate 15°

### D. Lateral movement:
- `D1`: move 10 cm left
- `D2`: move 10 cm right

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

Choose the next FSM state:

- `Reach Object`: if the object has not yet been reached or is still misaligned.
- `Describe Object`: if the object is centered, large, and visually close based on the above conditions.

---

## ⚠️ NOTES

- Avoid overshooting: stop once the object appears large and centered.
- Do not rotate if the object is already aligned.
