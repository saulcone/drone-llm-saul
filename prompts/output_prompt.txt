✅ Field Definitions

    "description": "... Step 4: SYMMETRY & DIAGONAL CHECK — Is one wall dominating the view? If the left wall is huge, angle is 'diagonal_from_left'. If right wall is huge, 'diagonal_from_right'. If perfectly symmetrical, 'perpendicular'. Step 5: PHASE C CHECK — If you are close AND diagonal, you MUST NOT advance (A1) or rotate. You MUST use lateral slides (D1 to slide left, D2 to slide right) to physically center your body. Step 6: CROSSING CHECK..."

    "door_position": Horizontal position of the candidate door opening in the image. Valid values: "left", "center", "right", "not_visible". This answers: WHERE is the door in the frame?

- `"door_angle"`: Perpendicularity and physical alignment assessment. Valid values:
    - `"perpendicular"`: SYMMETRICAL VIEW. The door is centered AND the walls/frames on the left and right take up an equal amount of visual space. You are physically aligned at 0 degrees or almost.
    - `"diagonal_from_left"`: You are physically standing too far to the left. Evidence: A large wall dominates the LEFT side of the image, or you see the inner depth of the right frame.
    - `"diagonal_from_right"`: You are physically standing too far to the right. Evidence: A large wall dominates the RIGHT side of the image, or you see the inner depth of the left frame.
    - `"unknown"`: Both frames visible but too far to judge inner depth or symmetry.

    "room": Current room from topological map, or "unknown".

    "state": Next FSM state. CRITICAL: Do NOT output Position in the center of room unless door_angle is perpendicular and the door occupies >60% of the visual frame. Otherwise, stay in Oriented Towards Door.

    "movement": Single command (A1, B1, etc.) chosen AFTER reasoning.

    "memory_update": A short note (max 200 chars) to your future self. Record landmarks, ignored doors, recognized rooms, obstacles, or any observation that will prevent you from repeating mistakes or re-exploring areas. If you receive an Agent Memory input, read it before deciding. If nothing new happened, copy the previous memory verbatim.

⚠️ Important

    Use all available context (image, state, previous movement, user query, and map) to ensure safe and meaningful output.
