## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL
Rotate to face the open door **perpendicularly** (door centered in frame), then move straight through it. Alignment is mandatory before entering — but "centered enough" means within the middle third of the frame.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Strict Priority)

🔁 **ZERO PRIORITY — ANTI-OSCILLATION**:
- Before anything else, check `mov_history`.
- If the last **3+ commands alternate `B1` and `C1`** without any `A1`, you are oscillating on a nearly-aligned door.
- **Stop. Return `A1` immediately.** The door is close enough to centered — go through it.

✅ **FIRST PRIORITY — ALIGNED: GO FORWARD**:
- If the door opening is **within the middle third of the frame** (roughly centered, not at the edges), the drone is facing the door perpendicularly.
- Return `A1` to move forward. Do NOT keep rotating for a perfect pixel-perfect center.

↔️ **SECOND PRIORITY — NOT ALIGNED: ROTATE TO CENTER**:
- If the door opening is clearly **in the left third of the frame** → rotate left: `B1`
- If the door opening is clearly **in the right third of the frame** → rotate right: `C1`
- Use only **one rotation step at a time**, then re-evaluate. This is how the drone gets perpendicular to the door.
- Use `B2`/`C2` (45°) only if the door is at the very edge or almost out of frame.

🚪 **THIRD PRIORITY — DOOR LOST**:
- If the open door is no longer visible in the frame, switch back to `Search open Door`.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

- `A1`: move forward 10 cm ← use when door is in the middle third of the frame
- `B1`: rotate 15° left ← use when door is in the left third
- `C1`: rotate 15° right ← use when door is in the right third
- `B2` / `C2`: rotate 45° ← only when door is nearly out of frame

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Go Through Door`: Door is centered and `A1` will cross it.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Door is visible but still in left/right third — still aligning.
- `Search open Door`: Door lost from view entirely.
