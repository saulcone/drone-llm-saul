## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL
Align until the open door is **centered in the frame AND you are facing it perpendicularly (head-on)**. Do NOT enter at a diagonal angle.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Strict Priority)

🚨 **PRIORITY 0: ABORT CLOSED OBJECTS**:
- If the visible object is a **CLOSED door, cabinet, or closet**, it is NOT the target.
- Switch to `Search open Door` and rotate away (`B2` or `C2`).

🔍 **PRIORITY 1: MULTIPLE DOORS RESOLUTION**:
- If there are MULTIPLE open doors visible, you MUST choose the one that leads to your target destination based on your user query.
- Ignore the wrong door completely.

🔁 **PRIORITY 1.5: ANTI-OSCILLATION**:
- Check `mov_history`. If your recent history alternates opposing commands (like B1/C1 or D1/D2 repeatedly), you are oscillating. **Return `A1` immediately.**

📐 **PRIORITY 2: PERPENDICULARITY CHECK (CRITICAL FIRST STEP)**:
- You must face the doorway perfectly straight-on (head-on), not from a diagonal side angle.
- **VISUAL CUE:** Look at the horizontal lines (ceiling/floor) and the doorframes. If the lines are slanted, or if you clearly see the deep "thickness" (inner depth) of a doorframe, you are at a diagonal angle.
- **ACTION:** You MUST use a small rotation to "square up" BEFORE sliding.
    - If you clearly see the inner depth of the LEFT doorframe, you are looking from the right side → rotate LEFT (`B1`).
    - If you clearly see the inner depth of the RIGHT doorframe, you are looking from the left side → rotate RIGHT (`C1`).
- **STRICT PROHIBITION:** Do NOT use `D1` or `D2` if your angle is skewed. Sliding laterally while diagonal will cause you to crash into the wall.

↔️ **PRIORITY 3: CENTERING (ONLY WHEN HEAD-ON)**:
- **If door opening is in the LEFT of the vision** → slide left: `D1`
- **If door opening is in the RIGHT of the vision** → slide right: `D2`
- **If door opening is in the CENTER of the vision** → you are perfectly aligned. Go to Priority 4.
- **PROHIBITED:** Never use `B1` or `C1` to center the door. Rotations are ONLY for fixing the diagonal angle and get completely perpendicular.

✅ **PRIORITY 4: CENTERED & PERPENDICULAR — ADVANCE**:
- Door is in the center of the vision, you are facing it head-on, AND it is an open void → return `A1`.
- This is the ONLY condition under which `A1` is allowed.

🚪 **PRIORITY 5: DOOR LOST**:
- If the open door is no longer visible, switch back to `Search open Door`, only if u didn't went through it, if it was like that, you need to explore before.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

- `A1`: move forward 10 cm ← **ONLY when centered AND perpendicular**
- `B1`: rotate 15° left ← **ONLY to fix a diagonal angle**
- `C1`: rotate 15° right ← **ONLY to fix a diagonal angle**
- `D1`: slide 5 cm left ← **ONLY to center the door when facing it head-on**
- `D2`: slide 5 cm right ← **ONLY to center the door when facing it head-on**
- `B2` / `C2`: rotate 45° ← abort only (door at edge or lost)

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Go Through Door`: Door is centered, you are perpendicular, and `A1` will cross it perfectly straight.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Door visible but needs centering or angle adjustment.
- `Search open Door`: Door is closed, is a cabinet, or is lost from view.
