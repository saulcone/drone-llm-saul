## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL
Align perfectly parallel to the target open door (0° angle), approach safely until close, and cross it cleanly.
- **Centering (Phase A)**: The door is in the horizontal middle of your view (rough centering is enough from afar).
- **Approaching (Phase B)**: Move forward to close the distance.
- **Perpendicularity & Crossing (Phase C)**: At close range, ensure both doorframe edges look identical (0° angle) before the final crossing step.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Strict Priority)

🛑 **RULE 0: SAFETY, ABORTS & OSCILLATION (CHECK FIRST)**:
- **Multiple Doors**: Use the topological map to pick the correct door. Ignore all others.
- **Oscillation Detection**: If you are caught in a rotation loop (e.g., C1 -> B1 -> C1) trying to center the door:
  - DO NOT rotate anymore.
  - If the door is roughly in the central third, emit `A1` to get closer.
  - If the door is clearly diagonal, use lateral slides (`D1` or `D2`) to break the loop and change the perspective.

🎯 **RULE 1: ROUGH CENTERING & APPROACH (FAR / MEDIUM DISTANCE)**:
- **When**: The door is far away.
- **Action**: Do not attempt perfect alignment from afar.
  | `door_position` | Action | Command |
  |---|---|---|
  | `left` | Rotate LEFT | `B1` or `B2` |
  | `right` | Rotate RIGHT | `C1` or `C2` |
  | `center` | **STRICTLY DO NOT ROTATE** | `A1` (advance) |

🚶 **RULE 2: DIAGONAL CORRECTION & ALIGNMENT (CLOSE RANGE)**:
- **When**: You are getting close, but the view is NOT symmetrical (e.g., a wall dominates the left or right side).
- **Action**: You are physically unaligned. **DO NOT rotate and DO NOT advance (A1).**
  - If `diagonal_from_left` (left wall is large): You must slide RIGHT using `D2`.
  - If `diagonal_from_right` (right wall is large): You must slide LEFT using `D1`.
- **Goal**: Slide until the walls on both sides of the door look symmetrical.

✅ **RULE 3: READY TO CROSS (PROXIMITY & PERPENDICULARITY)**:
- You are **ONLY** ready to cross if BOTH conditions are met:
  1. **Perpendicular (0 degrees)**: The view is completely symmetrical.
  2. **Proximity**: The open void occupies >60% of the image width.
- **Action**: Emit `A1` to cross.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

- `A1`: move forward 10 cm (Default approach / cross command)
- `B1`: rotate 15° left (Micro-correct drift / face left door)
- `B2`: rotate 45° left (Face far left door)
- `C1`: rotate 15° right (Micro-correct drift / face right door)
- `C2`: rotate 45° right (Face far right door)
- `D1`: slide 5 cm left (Diagonal correction. Breaks rotation loops)
- `D2`: slide 5 cm right (Diagonal correction. Breaks rotation loops)

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Oriented Towards Door`: **DEFAULT STATE**. You must remain in this state while centering, approaching (even if emitting A1), or adjusting angles.
- `Position in the center of room`: **STRICT LOCK**. You may ONLY transition to this state if in the CURRENT step the door is `perpendicular`, it occupies >60% of the view, and you are executing `A1` to cross the threshold.
- `Search open Door`: Target is closed, lost, or aborted.
