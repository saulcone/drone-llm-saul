## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Align the robot directly in front of an OPEN door without getting too close and losing visual context.

---

## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Strict Priority)

⚠️ **ZERO PRIORITY — ANTI-OSCILLATION (CHECK THIS FIRST)**:
- Inspect `mov_history`. If the last **3 or more** commands contain alternating rotations (`B1`/`C1` mixed, or the same rotation repeated), you are **stuck in a loop**.
- **IMMEDIATELY return `A1`** — the alignment is good enough, commit to crossing.
- This rule overrides everything below.

🚨 **FIRST PRIORITY — OBSTACLE ON ONE SIDE**:
- If there is a FIREPLACE, COLUMN, or LARGE OBJECT **very close** to your left or right:
  - Do NOT move laterally towards it (`D1`/`D2`).
  - Use small rotations (`B1`/`C1`) to re-angle, then forward (`A1`).
  - If it is blocking the path entirely, retreat with `B2` or `C2`.

1️⃣ **ALIGNMENT — PREFER LATERAL SLIDING (DEFAULT METHOD)**:
- The goal is to keep the door centered in the frame **without rotating the camera away from it**.
- If the door is slightly off-center:
  - Door on the **left** → slide left: `D1`
  - Door on the **right** → slide right: `D2`
- Use `D1`/`D2` even for small offsets. This keeps the camera pointing at the door the whole time.
- **Only use rotations (`B1`/`C1`) when** the door is very far to the side (more than half the frame width) AND lateral sliding is blocked by an obstacle.

2️⃣ **FORWARD COMMIT — When aligned enough**:
- If the door is roughly centered (within ~1/4 of the frame from center) AND occupies a large portion of the frame, return `A1` to start crossing.
- Do NOT keep adjusting for pixel-perfect alignment.

3️⃣ **FIELD OF VIEW PRESERVATION**:
- Do not get so close to the door frame that you lose visual context. If the frame edge fills more than 1/3 of the image, slide away from it (`D1` or `D2`).

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return **ONE** movement command from the list below:

### A. Forward movement:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm

### B. Left rotation:
- `B1`: rotate 15°
- `B2`: rotate 45° (Use ONLY for aborting)

### C. Right rotation:
- `C1`: rotate 15°
- `C2`: rotate 45° (Use ONLY for aborting)

### D. Lateral movement:
- `D1`: move 10 cm left  
- `D2`: move 10 cm right

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Go Through Door`: Open door is centered and close enough to cross.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Open door is visible but needs alignment or safe approach.
- `Search open Door`: Door is CLOSED, is a cabinet, or is lost from view.
