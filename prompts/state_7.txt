## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL
Rotate until the open door is **centered in the frame** (perpendicular approach), then advance straight through it. Do NOT enter at an angle.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Strict Priority)

🚨 **PRIORITY 0: ABORT CLOSED OBJECTS**:
- If the visible object is a **CLOSED door, cabinet, or closet**, it is NOT the target.
- Switch to `Search open Door` and rotate away (`B2` or `C2`).

🔁 **PRIORITY 1: ANTI-OSCILLATION**:
- Check `mov_history`. If the last **3+ commands alternate `B1` and `C1`** with no `A1` between them, you are oscillating.
- The door is close enough to centered. **Return `A1` immediately.**

↔️ **PRIORITY 2: ALIGNMENT (MANDATORY BEFORE ADVANCING)**:
- Divide the frame into three equal vertical thirds: left / center / right.
- **If door opening is in the LEFT third** → rotate left: `B1`
- **If door opening is in the RIGHT third** → rotate right: `C1`
- **If door opening is in the CENTER third** → the drone is perpendicular. Go to Priority 3.
- Use `B2`/`C2` (45°) only if the door is at the very edge or almost out of frame.
- **PROHIBITED: Never use `A1` if the door is in the left or right third. You will hit the doorframe.**

✅ **PRIORITY 3: CENTERED — ADVANCE**:
- Door is in the center third AND is an open void → return `A1`.
- This is the ONLY condition under which `A1` is allowed.

🚪 **PRIORITY 4: DOOR LOST**:
- If the open door is no longer visible in the frame, switch back to `Search open Door`.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

- `A1`: move forward 10 cm ← **ONLY when door is in the center third**
- `B1`: rotate 15° left ← when door is in the left third
- `C1`: rotate 15° right ← when door is in the right third
- `B2` / `C2`: rotate 45° ← abort only (door at edge or lost)

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Go Through Door`: Door is in the center third and `A1` will cross it perpendicularly.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Door visible but in left/right third — still rotating to align.
- `Search open Door`: Door is closed, is a cabinet, or is lost from view.
