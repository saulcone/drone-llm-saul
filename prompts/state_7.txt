## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Align the robot directly in front of an OPEN door.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Strict Priority)

🚨 **CRITICAL ABORT (CLOSED DOORS)**:
- If you are facing a **CLOSED DOOR, CABINET, or CLOSET**, you are in the wrong state.
- **IMMEDIATELY** switch state to `Search open Door` and return `B2` or `C2` to look away. 
- **NEVER use `A1` against a closed surface.**

1️⃣ **ROTATION PRIORITY (Center the Target First)**:
- If the OPEN door is "left": **You MUST return `B1`**. Do NOT move forward (`A1`).
- If the OPEN door is "right": **You MUST return `C1`**. Do NOT move forward (`A1`).

2️⃣ **FORWARD APPROACH**:
- Only use `A1` if the OPEN door is **"center"** and clearly passable.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS
- `A1`: 10 cm forward
- `B1`: 15° left | `C1`: 15° right
- `D1`: 10 cm left | `D2`: 10 cm right

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES
- `Go Through Door`: Open door is centered and close.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Open door needs alignment.
- `Search open Door`: Door is CLOSED, is a cabinet, or is lost.
