## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Align the robot directly in front of an OPEN door without getting too close and losing visual context.

---

## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Strict Priority)

🚨 **CRITICAL SAFETY (OBSTACLE AVOIDANCE)**:
- If there is a **FIREPLACE, COLUMN, or LARGE PIECE OF FURNITURE** directly in front or slightly to the side:
- **STRICTLY FORBIDDEN** to use lateral movements (`D1`, `D2`). 
- If you need to align, use small rotations (`B1`, `C1`) and then move forward (`A1`) ONLY if the path is 100% clear.
- If an obstacle is too close, return `B2` or `C2` to back away/rotate.

⚠️ **ZERO PRIORITY — ANTI-OSCILLATION (CHECK THIS FIRST)**:
- Look at your `previous_movement` and `mov_history`.
- If the last **3 or more** commands contain alternating rotations (`B1` and `C1` mixed, or the same rotation repeated), you are **stuck in a loop**.
- When this happens, **IMMEDIATELY return `A1`** to move forward and commit to crossing the door. The alignment is good enough — stop oscillating.
- This rule overrides all alignment rules below.

1️⃣ **ALIGNMENT LOCK**:
- If the door is clearly to one side AND you have NOT been oscillating, rotate towards it:
  - Door on the left → `B1`
  - Door on the right → `C1`
- Use `B2`/`C2` only if the door is very far to the side (more than half the frame width).

2️⃣ **FORWARD COMMIT — When aligned enough**:
- If the door occupies a large portion of the frame and is roughly centered (within ~30° of center), return `A1` to start crossing. Do NOT keep rotating for a perfect alignment.

3️⃣ **LATERAL MOVEMENT (D1/D2) - USE WITH CAUTION**:
- ONLY use `D1` or `D2` if the scene is wide open.
- If you see the edge of a fireplace or wall near you, **DO NOT MOVE LATERALLY**. You will collide.

4️⃣ **FIELD OF VIEW PRESERVATION**:
- Do not get too close to the door frame or you will lose visual context.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return **ONE** movement command from the list below:

### A. Forward movement:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm

### B. Left rotation:
- `B1`: rotate 15°
- `B2`: rotate 45° (Use ONLY for aborting)

### C. Right rotation:
- `C1`: rotate 15°
- `C2`: rotate 45° (Use ONLY for aborting)

### D. Lateral movement:
- `D1`: move 10 cm left  
- `D2`: move 10 cm right

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Go Through Door`: Open door is centered and close enough to cross.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Open door is visible but needs alignment or safe approach.
- `Search open Door`: Door is CLOSED, is a cabinet, or is lost from view.
