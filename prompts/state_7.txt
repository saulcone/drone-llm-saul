## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Align the robot directly in front of an open door and approach it safely.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return **ONE** movement command from the list below:

### A. Forward movement:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm

### B. Left rotation:
- `B1`: rotate 15°

### C. Right rotation:
- `C1`: rotate 15°

### D. Lateral movement:
- `D1`: move 10 cm left  
- `D2`: move 10 cm right

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (priority ordered)

1️⃣ **ROTATION PRIORITY (Center the Target First)**:
   - If `door_position` is "left": **You MUST use `B1`**. Do NOT move forward.
   - If `door_position` is "right": **You MUST use `C1`**. Do NOT move forward.
   - **Reasoning**: Moving forward while the door is on the side causes the robot to miss the entrance or hit the wall next to it. Align first.

2️⃣ **LATERAL CORRECTION**:
   - Use `D1`/`D2` ONLY if the door is "center" but you are physically offset (parallel shift), and you are close.

3️⃣ **FORWARD APPROACH**:
   - Only use `A1` if `door_position` is **"center"**.
   - If the door is centered and looks open/void, advance confidently.

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

Choose the next FSM state based on door position and visual characteristics:

- `Go Through Door`: if the door is centered (`door_position = "center"`) and appears  close (large, cropped top/bottom) or if the door is not visible and the previous movement was `E`
- `Oriented Towards Door`: if the door is visible but not yet centered or  close
- `Search open Door`: if the door disappears for 2 or more consecutive steps

---

## ⚠️ NOTES

- Do not endlessly alternate between `D1` and `D2`.
- Do not endlessly alternate between `C1` and `C2`.
- If the door is stable in the frame (left or right) and appears  close, stop correcting and proceed forward.
- Never stay in this state if you are  close and almost aligned — act confidently to exit.
- Always respect the priority order: 1️⃣ > 2️⃣ > 3️⃣.
