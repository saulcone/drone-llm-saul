## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL
Get close to the target open door and align with it so you can cross it cleanly. This is a **two-phase** process:
1. **Far away** → advance toward the door (A1), even if it's not perfectly centered yet. Lateral fine-tuning is useless at distance.
2. **Close up** (doorframe sides clearly visible and large) → stop advancing and fine-tune centering and angle.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Strict Priority)

🚨 **PRIORITY 0: ABORT CLOSED OBJECTS**:
- If the visible object is a **CLOSED door, cabinet, or closet**, it is NOT the target.
- Switch to `Search open Door` and rotate away (`B2` or `C2`).

🔍 **PRIORITY 1: MULTIPLE DOORS RESOLUTION**:
- If there are MULTIPLE open doors visible, you MUST select the correct one using the **topological map**.
- Look at the room connections in the map: identify which adjacent room matches your objective.
- Examine what's **visible through each door** (flooring, wall color, objects) to match it to the target room in the map.
- Once selected, treat only that door as the target and ignore all others completely.

🔁 **PRIORITY 1.5: OSCILLATION DETECTION**:
- Check `mov_history`. If it contains **any alternation of `B1` and `C1`**, you are in a rotation loop. Stop rotating — slide toward the door side instead (`D1`/`D2`).
- If it contains **3+ consecutive identical lateral slides** (`D1` or `D2`) and the door has NOT moved closer to center, the door is still far away. **Switch to Priority 2 (approach first).**

🚀 **PRIORITY 2: APPROACH PHASE (DOOR IS DISTANT)**:
- **How to tell you are far**: The door opening appears **small** in the frame, and/or the doorframe edges are **thin**, and/or you can see a lot of room/wall around the door.
- **When far away, lateral slides (D1/D2) of 5 cm are nearly useless** — the door barely moves in the image. Do NOT waste steps on repeated D1/D2 at distance.
- **ACTION**: As long as the door is visible anywhere in the frame (left, center, or right) and the path ahead is not blocked, return `A1` to **advance toward it**. Getting closer is the priority.
- **Edge-of-frame exception**: If the door is about to disappear off the edge of the frame, do ONE lateral slide to keep it visible, then resume `A1`:
  - Door disappearing off the **LEFT** edge → slide LEFT `D1` (this moves you left, shifting the view so the door stays in frame).
  - Door disappearing off the **RIGHT** edge → slide RIGHT `D2`.
  - **REMEMBER**: Slide in the SAME direction as the door's position. Door on left = `D1`. Door on right = `D2`. This is because sliding moves your body, not the camera direction.
- **Stay in this phase** until the doorframe sides become **clearly visible and large** (filling a significant portion of the image height and the door opening is wide in the frame).

↔️ **PRIORITY 3: LATERAL CENTERING (ONLY WHEN CLOSE)**:
- You are close when the doorframe sides are clearly visible and the door opening is large in the frame.
- **Now** use lateral slides to bring the door to center:
  - Door to the LEFT → `D1`. Door to the RIGHT → `D2`. Door in CENTER → go to Priority 4.
- **Critical reasoning**: When the door is off to one side, you will see the inner depth of the nearer frame — this is lateral displacement, NOT a rotation angle. Do NOT use B1/C1 to fix it.
- **Never rotate (B1/C1) when the door is off-center.** Lateral slides preserve your angle; rotations corrupt it.

📐 **PRIORITY 4: PERPENDICULARITY CHECK (ONLY WHEN CLOSE AND CENTERED)**:
- Only check this once the door is roughly centered AND you are close.
- **VISUAL CUE:** Horizontal lines (ceiling/floor) inside the doorway are slanted, or you see the deep "thickness" of one doorframe **while centered** → you are at a diagonal angle.
- **Rotation loop escape**: 3+ consecutive same-direction B1/C1 → assume close enough, proceed to Priority 5.
- **ACTION:** Single small rotation to square up:
    - Inner depth of LEFT frame visible → rotate LEFT (`B1`).
    - Inner depth of RIGHT frame visible → rotate RIGHT (`C1`).
- After any B1/C1: re-evaluate both centering and angle from scratch.

✅ **PRIORITY 5: CENTERED, PERPENDICULAR & CLOSE — ADVANCE TO CROSS**:
- Door is centered, you are facing it head-on, it is an open void, and you are close → return `A1`.
- **Transition to `Go Through Door`** when the doorframe sides fill a large portion of the view and you are about to enter.

🚪 **PRIORITY 6: DOOR LOST**:
- If the open door is no longer visible, switch back to `Search open Door`, only if you didn't go through it. If you already crossed it, transition to `Position in the center of room` to reorient in the new room.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

- `A1`: move forward 10 cm ← **To approach a distant door, OR to cross when close, centered & perpendicular**
- `B1`: rotate 15° left ← **ONLY to fix a diagonal angle when close and centered**
- `C1`: rotate 15° right ← **ONLY to fix a diagonal angle when close and centered**
- `D1`: slide 5 cm left ← **To keep door in frame while approaching, or to center when close**
- `D2`: slide 5 cm right ← **To keep door in frame while approaching, or to center when close**
- `B2` / `C2`: rotate 45° ← abort only (door at edge or lost)

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Go Through Door`: Door is centered, you are perpendicular, and `A1` will cross it perfectly straight.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Door visible but needs centering or angle adjustment.
- `Search open Door`: Door is closed, is a cabinet, or is lost from view.
