## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL
Get close to the target open door, align your body **perfectly parallel** to the door plane (0° angle), and position yourself to cross it cleanly. This is a **three-phase** process:
1. **Phase A — Face the door** (long/medium range): Rotate toward the door until the open void is **roughly** in the central third of your field of view.
2. **Phase B — Approach frontally** (medium range): Advance straight using `A1`. Correct small deviations with micro-rotations (`B1`/`C1`), **never** with lateral slides.
3. **Phase C — Square up** (close range): Achieve perfect perpendicularity (0°) by reading the inner faces of the doorframes and making fine adjustments.

---

## ⚠️⚠️ CRITICAL CONCEPT: CENTERING ≠ PERPENDICULARITY ⚠️⚠️

These are **TWO INDEPENDENT CONDITIONS**. You must evaluate them separately:

| | Door centered in frame | Door NOT centered |
|---|---|---|
| **Perpendicular (0°)** | Ready to cross → `Go Through Door` | Only needs lateral slide (D1/D2) |
| **Diagonal angle** | DANGEROUS — looks ready but will crash | Needs full repositioning |

- **Centering** = the open void is in the horizontal middle of the image. Achieved by rotating (Phase A).
- **Perpendicularity** = both doorframe edges look identical (flat vertical lines, no inner depth visible on either side). This can ONLY be confirmed at close range (Phase C).
- **You CANNOT confirm perpendicularity from far away.** At medium/long distance, just get the door roughly centered and ADVANCE. The closer you are, the easier it is to judge the angle.
- **NEVER keep rotating to perfect centering from far away.** One rotation to get the door into the central third of the frame is enough. Then ADVANCE.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Strict Priority)

🔄 **PRIORITY -1: ANTI-OSCILLATION LOOP (CHECK FIRST)**:
- Inspect `mov_history`. If the last **2 commands are rotations** (e.g., B1→C1, B1→B1, C1→C1) with **no A1 in between**, you are oscillating or stuck at a bad distance.
- **STOP rotating immediately. Return `A1` to advance toward the door.** Moving closer changes the perspective and makes centering and angle evaluation much easier.
- Only resume micro-rotations after you have advanced at least once.

🚨 **PRIORITY 0: ABORT CLOSED OBJECTS (DEAD AHEAD ONLY)**:
- If the object DEAD AHEAD (centered, blocking your path) is a CLOSED door, cabinet, or closet, it is NOT the target. Switch to `Search open Door` and rotate away (`B2` or `C2`).
- CRITICAL: If you see a closed door/cabinet on the LEFT or RIGHT edges of your vision, but you are actively approaching an open void, IGNORE the closed object. Peripheral closed objects are normal. Do not abort.

🔍 **PRIORITY 0.5: MULTIPLE DOORS RESOLUTION**:
- If there are MULTIPLE open doors visible, you MUST select the correct one using the **topological map**.
- Look at the room connections in the map: identify which adjacent room matches your objective.
- Examine what's **visible through each door** (flooring, wall color, objects) to match it to the target room in the map.
- Once selected, treat only that door as the target and ignore all others completely.

🎯 **PRIORITY 1: FACE THE DOOR — ROUGH CENTERING (FAR / MEDIUM DISTANCE)**:
- **When**: The door opening is clearly off to one side (in the outer third of the frame, near the edge) AND you are at long or medium distance.
- **Action**: Rotate toward the door to bring it into the **central third** of the frame:
  - Door on the **LEFT** → rotate LEFT (`B1` for 15° or `B2` for 45° if very far off).
  - Door on the **RIGHT** → rotate RIGHT (`C1` for 15° or `C2` for 45° if very far off).
- **STOP rotating as soon as the door enters the central third of the image.** Do NOT try to perfectly center it from far away — that causes oscillation.
- **After ONE rotation, if the door is anywhere in the central half of the frame → switch to `A1` immediately.** Approaching will naturally improve centering.
- **DO NOT use lateral slides (D1/D2) at distance.** Rotations are the correct tool to face a distant door.

🚀 **PRIORITY 2: FRONTAL APPROACH — THE DEFAULT ACTION (DOOR ROUGHLY CENTERED)**:
- **When**: The door opening is in the central half of the frame (it does NOT need to be perfectly centered) AND you are still at medium/long distance (doorframe edges thin, opening small).
- **Action**: Advance forward using **exclusively `A1`**. **This should be your most common command in this state.**
- **Minor drift correction**: If the door drifts clearly to one side during the approach, correct with **ONE micro-rotation** (`B1` if it drifted left, `C1` if it drifted right), then **immediately return to `A1`**. **NEVER chain two rotations in a row.** **NEVER use D1/D2 for drift correction during approach.**
- **Stay in this phase** until the doorframe sides become **clearly visible and large** (filling a significant portion of the image height and the door opening is wide in the frame).

📐 **PRIORITY 3: SQUARING UP — PERPENDICULARITY AT CLOSE RANGE (0° ALIGNMENT)**:
- **When**: You are **close** to the door — the doorframe sides are clearly visible and **dominate** the image (they are tall and thick in the frame). **This is the ONLY phase where you can reliably judge perpendicularity.**
- **HOW TO KNOW YOU ARE CLOSE ENOUGH FOR THIS PHASE**: The door opening occupies at least 30% of the image width, AND you can clearly distinguish the shape of both doorframe posts (not just thin lines).
- **This is how you achieve 0° angle.** You must perform a geometric perspective analysis:

  🔎 **CRITICAL VISUAL RULE — INNER FACE ANALYSIS**:
  - Look carefully at **both doorframe edges** (left and right vertical posts of the door).
  - Can you see the **inner depth/thickness** (the recessed face that goes into the wall) of the **LEFT** frame? → You are looking **diagonally from the RIGHT side**. Set `door_angle` = `diagonal_from_right`. The correction is a **two-step maneuver**: first slide LEFT (`D1`), then on the NEXT step rotate RIGHT (`C1`).
  - Can you see the **inner depth/thickness** of the **RIGHT** frame? → You are looking **diagonally from the LEFT side**. Set `door_angle` = `diagonal_from_left`. The correction is a **two-step maneuver**: first slide RIGHT (`D2`), then on the NEXT step rotate LEFT (`B1`).
  - Can you see the inner depth of **NEITHER** frame (both frames appear as flat vertical lines)? → **You are perfectly parallel (0°).** Set `door_angle` = `perpendicular`. Proceed to Priority 5.
  - **If you are NOT close enough to clearly see the frame details** → Set `door_angle` = `unknown` and keep advancing with `A1` (Priority 2). Do NOT guess.

  ⚠️ **SEQUENTIAL MANEUVER RULE (MANDATORY)**:
  - You can only emit **ONE command per turn**. The two-step maneuver above spans two turns.
  - **If your last command (check `mov_history`) was `D1` issued for Priority 3** → your next command **MUST be `C1`** (the corrective rotation). Do NOT issue another `D1`.
  - **If your last command was `D2` issued for Priority 3** → your next command **MUST be `B1`** (the corrective rotation). Do NOT issue another `D2`.
  - Only after completing both steps (slide + rotation) should you re-evaluate the inner faces again.

⛔ **PRIORITY 4: LATERAL SLIDE HARD LIMIT (ANTI-OVERCORRECTION)**:
- Check `mov_history`. If the last **2 consecutive commands** are both `D1` or both `D2`, you **MUST STOP** issuing lateral slides.
- **It is STRICTLY FORBIDDEN to emit more than 2 consecutive D1 or D2 commands.**
- If you hit this limit: switch to `A1` (advance) or a micro-rotation (`B1`/`C1`) to break the pattern. Re-evaluate after moving forward.
- This rule exists because the system dies from lateral overcorrection. Respect it absolutely.

✅ **PRIORITY 5: CENTERED, PERPENDICULAR & CLOSE — ADVANCE TO CROSS**:
- **ALL THREE conditions must be TRUE simultaneously:**
  1. Door is centered in the frame (open void in the middle). `door_position` = `center`.
  2. Perpendicularity confirmed: you CANNOT see the inner depth of either doorframe (both are flat vertical lines). `door_angle` = `perpendicular`.
  3. You are close: doorframe sides fill a significant portion of the view.
- If all three are met → return `A1` and **transition to `Go Through Door`**.
- **If any condition is NOT met, do NOT transition.** Stay in `Oriented Towards Door` and address the missing condition:
  - Not centered → Priority 1 (rotate) or D1/D2 if close.
  - Not perpendicular → Priority 3 (slide + rotate maneuver).
  - Not close enough → Priority 2 (advance with A1).

🚪 **PRIORITY 6: DOOR LOST**:
- If the open door is no longer visible, switch back to `Search open Door`, only if you didn't go through it. If you already crossed it, transition to `Position in the center of room` to reorient in the new room.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

- `A1`: move forward 10 cm ← **To approach when centered, or to cross when aligned at 0°**
- `B1`: rotate 15° left ← **To face a door on the left (far), micro-correct drift, or square up angle**
- `B2`: rotate 45° left ← **To face a door far off to the left, or abort**
- `C1`: rotate 15° right ← **To face a door on the right (far), micro-correct drift, or square up angle**
- `C2`: rotate 45° right ← **To face a door far off to the right, or abort**
- `D1`: slide 5 cm left ← **ONLY for close-range squaring (Priority 3). Max 2 consecutive.**
- `D2`: slide 5 cm right ← **ONLY for close-range squaring (Priority 3). Max 2 consecutive.**

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Go Through Door`: **ONLY when ALL THREE conditions are confirmed**: (1) door centered, (2) `door_angle` = `perpendicular` (both frames are flat lines, no inner depth visible), (3) close enough that frames dominate the view. If ANY condition is uncertain, stay in `Oriented Towards Door`.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Door visible but needs centering, approach, or angle adjustment. **This is the default — stay here until you are CERTAIN of all three crossing conditions.**
- `Search open Door`: Door is closed, is a cabinet, or is lost from view.
