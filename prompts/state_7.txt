## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## GOAL
Get close to the target open door, align your body **perfectly parallel** to the door plane (0 degree angle), and position yourself to cross it cleanly. This is a **three-phase** process:
1. **Phase A -- Face the door** (long/medium range): Rotate toward the door until the open void is **roughly** in the central third of your field of view.
2. **Phase B -- Once in the centre of the view, check if perfectly parallel, if not (diagonal) try to rotate to accomplish.
3. **Phase C -- Once perpendicular to the door, start to centre the door again in the view by sliding to the needed direction (`D1` if door at left, `D2` if it is at the right).


## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL
Align perfectly parallel to the target open door (0° angle) and cross it. 
- **Centering**: The door is in the horizontal middle of your view. Achieved by rotating (Phase A).
- **Perpendicularity (0°)**: Both doorframe edges look identical (flat vertical lines, no inner depth visible). Achieved by lateral sliding + micro-rotating (Phase C).

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Strict Priority)

🛑 **RULE 0: SAFETY, ABORTS & OSCILLATION (CHECK FIRST)**:
- **Multiple Doors**: Use the topological map to pick the correct door. Ignore all others.

🎯 **RULE 1: ROUGH CENTERING (FAR / MEDIUM DISTANCE)**:
- **When**: The door is clearly off to one side (outer third of frame).
- **Action**: Rotate to bring it into the central third, then STOP rotating and advance (`A1`).
  | `door_position` | Rotation | Command |
  |---|---|---|
  | `left` | Rotate LEFT | `B1` or `B2` |
  | `right` | Rotate RIGHT | `C1` or `C2` |
  | `center` | Do NOT rotate| `A1` (advance) |

🚶 **RULE 2: FRONTAL APPROACH**:
- **When**: Door is roughly centered, but you are still far.
- **Action**: Advance with `A1` **ONLY** if possible without crashing.
- **Edge Check**: Before advancing, verify you can see BOTH doorframe edges. If you only see ONE edge  you are not perfectly perpendicular -> try to slide the opposite way the needed times and turn (opposite way of the slide) to check now perspective.

✅ **RULE 3: READY TO CROSS**:
- Check if angle is perfectly 0 degrees to the door's wall.
- Check if doorframe is centered perfectly or almost perfectly with the view.
- Check if moving forward is possible, then do it to cross.

❓ **RULE 4: DOOR LOST**:
- Once you crossed the door, switch to **Position in the center of room** OR **Search object**.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

- `A1`: move forward 10 cm (Default approach / cross command)
- `B1`: rotate 15° left (Micro-correct drift / face left door)
- `B2`: rotate 45° left (Face far left door)
- `C1`: rotate 15° right (Micro-correct drift / face right door)
- `C2`: rotate 45° right (Face far right door)
- `D1`: slide 5 cm left (Diagonal correction ONLY. Max 2 consecutive)
- `D2`: slide 5 cm right (Diagonal correction ONLY. Max 2 consecutive)

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Go Through Door`: **ONLY** when door is centered, perfectly perpendicular (0°), and close.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Default state. Door is visible but needs centering, approach, or angle adjustment.
- `Search open Door`: Target is closed, lost, or aborted.
- `Position in the center of room`: You successfully crossed the threshold into the new room.
