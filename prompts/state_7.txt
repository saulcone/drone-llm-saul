## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## GOAL
Get close to the target open door, align your body **perfectly parallel** to the door plane (0 degree angle), and position yourself to cross it cleanly. This is a **three-phase** process:
1. **Phase A -- Face the door** (long/medium range): Rotate toward the door until the open void is **roughly** in the central third of your field of view.
2. **Phase B -- Approach frontally** (medium range): Advance straight using `A1`. Correct small deviations with micro-rotations (`B1`/`C1`), **never** with lateral slides.
3. **Phase C -- Square up** (close range): Achieve perfect perpendicularity (0 degrees) by reading the inner faces of the doorframes and making fine adjustments.

---

## CRITICAL CONCEPT: CENTERING IS NOT THE SAME AS PERPENDICULARITY

These are **TWO INDEPENDENT CONDITIONS**. You must evaluate them separately:

| | Door centered in frame | Door NOT centered |
|---|---|---|
| **Perpendicular (0 deg)** | Ready to cross -> `Go Through Door` | Only needs lateral slide (D1/D2) |
| **Diagonal angle** | DANGEROUS -- looks ready but will crash | Needs full repositioning |

- **Centering** = the open void is in the horizontal middle of the image. Achieved by rotating (Phase A).
- **Perpendicularity** = both doorframe edges look identical (flat vertical lines, no inner depth visible on either side). Can ONLY be confirmed when you can see both frame edges clearly.
- **NEVER keep rotating to perfect centering from far away.** One rotation to get the door into the central third of the frame is enough. Then ADVANCE.

---

## POLICY RULES (Strict Priority)

**PRIORITY -1: ANTI-OSCILLATION LOOP (CHECK FIRST)**:
- Inspect `mov_history`. If the last **2 commands are rotations** (e.g., B1->C1, B1->B1, C1->C1) with **no A1 in between**, you are oscillating or stuck at a bad distance.
- **STOP rotating immediately. Return `A1` to advance toward the door.** Moving closer changes the perspective and makes centering and angle evaluation much easier.
- Only resume micro-rotations after you have advanced at least once.

**PRIORITY 0: ABORT CLOSED OBJECTS (DEAD AHEAD ONLY)**:
- If the object DEAD AHEAD (centered, blocking your path) is a CLOSED door, cabinet, or closet, it is NOT the target. Switch to `Search open Door` and rotate away (`B2` or `C2`).
- CRITICAL: If you see a closed door/cabinet on the LEFT or RIGHT edges of your vision, but you are actively approaching an open void, IGNORE the closed object. Peripheral closed objects are normal. Do not abort.

**PRIORITY 0.5: MULTIPLE DOORS RESOLUTION**:
- If there are MULTIPLE open doors visible, you MUST select the correct one using the **topological map**.
- Look at the room connections in the map: identify which adjacent room matches your objective.
- Examine what is visible through each door (flooring, wall color, objects) to match it to the target room in the map.
- Once selected, treat only that door as the target and ignore all others completely.

**PRIORITY 1: FACE THE DOOR -- ROUGH CENTERING (FAR / MEDIUM DISTANCE)**:
- **When**: The door opening is clearly off to one side (in the outer third of the frame, near the edge) AND you are at long or medium distance.
- **Action**: Rotate toward the door to bring it into the **central third** of the frame.

  **DIRECTION TABLE (ABSOLUTE -- MEMORIZE THIS):**
  | `door_position` | Rotation direction | Valid commands |
  |---|---|---|
  | `left` | Rotate LEFT | `B1` or `B2` |
  | `right` | Rotate RIGHT | `C1` or `C2` |
  | `center` | Do NOT rotate | `A1` (advance) |

  **VALIDATION (CHECK EVERY TIME):** Before emitting your command, verify:
  - If `door_position` = `right` and your command starts with `B` -> **WRONG. Change to C.**
  - If `door_position` = `left` and your command starts with `C` -> **WRONG. Change to B.**
  - Logic: B = body turns LEFT = camera pans LEFT = objects move RIGHT in frame. So to move a door from RIGHT toward center, rotate RIGHT = C.

- **STOP rotating as soon as the door enters the central third of the image.** Do NOT try to perfectly center it from far away -- that causes oscillation.
- **After ONE rotation, if the door is anywhere in the central half of the frame -> switch to `A1` immediately.** Approaching will naturally improve centering.
- **DO NOT use lateral slides (D1/D2) at distance.** Rotations are the correct tool to face a distant door.

**PRIORITY 2: FRONTAL APPROACH (DOOR ROUGHLY CENTERED)**:
- **When**: The door opening is in the central half of the frame AND you are still at medium/long distance.

  **MANDATORY FRAME-EDGE CHECK (DO THIS BEFORE EVERY `A1`):**
  Before advancing, you MUST explicitly answer: **"Can I see the LEFT doorframe edge? Can I see the RIGHT doorframe edge?"**
  - **Both edges visible** (even if thin): `door_angle` = `unknown` -> safe to advance with `A1`.
  - **Only ONE edge visible** (the other side is a wall or off-screen): **You are approaching diagonally. Set `door_angle` accordingly and STOP -- go to Priority 2.5.**
    - Wall on the LEFT, only LEFT frame edge visible -> `door_angle` = `diagonal_from_left`.
    - Wall on the RIGHT, only RIGHT frame edge visible -> `door_angle` = `diagonal_from_right`.
  - **Neither edge visible** (only the open void, no frame edges at all): You are far away and roughly centered -> safe to advance with `A1`.

- **If `door_angle` = `unknown` or no edges visible**: Advance forward using `A1`. This should be your most common command.
- **Minor drift correction**: If the door drifts to one side, correct with **ONE micro-rotation** (`B1` if drifted LEFT, `C1` if drifted RIGHT -- sanity check: door LEFT = B, door RIGHT = C), then immediately `A1`. **NEVER chain two rotations.**
- **Stay in this phase** until the doorframe sides become clearly visible and large.

**PRIORITY 2.5: DIAGONAL APPROACH DETECTED -- STOP AND CORRECT**:
- **When**: `door_angle` is `diagonal_from_left` or `diagonal_from_right` (detected via one-edge-visible check, inner face analysis, or from memory).
- **CRITICAL: If you are approaching diagonally, advancing with `A1` will crash you into the wall next to the door. You MUST correct your lateral position BEFORE advancing further.**
- **Action -- immediate lateral correction:**
  - `diagonal_from_left` (wall on your left) -> slide RIGHT (`D2`), then next turn rotate LEFT (`B1`).
  - `diagonal_from_right` (wall on your right) -> slide LEFT (`D1`), then next turn rotate RIGHT (`C1`).
- **After the 2-step correction**, re-evaluate: if both frame edges are now visible, resume advancing (Priority 2). If still diagonal, repeat correction.
- **Memory**: Note in `memory_update` which side the wall is on.

**PRIORITY 3: SQUARING UP -- PERPENDICULARITY CHECK (CLOSE RANGE)**:
- **When**: You are **close** to the door -- doorframe sides are clearly visible and dominate the image, AND `door_angle` is still `unknown`.
- **HOW TO KNOW YOU ARE CLOSE**: The door opening occupies at least 30% of the image width.
- Perform the inner face analysis:

  **INNER FACE ANALYSIS**:
  - **LEFT frame inner depth visible** -> `door_angle` = `diagonal_from_right` -> go to Priority 2.5 correction.
  - **RIGHT frame inner depth visible** -> `door_angle` = `diagonal_from_left` -> go to Priority 2.5 correction.
  - **NEITHER inner face visible** (both are flat vertical lines) -> `door_angle` = `perpendicular` -> go to Priority 5.
  - **Only ONE frame edge visible** (other side is wall) -> same partial-view rule: set `door_angle` accordingly and apply 2-step correction from Priority 2.5.

  **SEQUENTIAL MANEUVER RULE (MANDATORY)**:
  - You can only emit **ONE command per turn**. The slide+rotation maneuver spans two turns.
  - **If your last command was `D1`** -> your next command **MUST be `C1`**. Do NOT issue another `D1`.
  - **If your last command was `D2`** -> your next command **MUST be `B1`**. Do NOT issue another `D2`.
  - Only after completing both steps should you re-evaluate.

**PRIORITY 4: LATERAL SLIDE HARD LIMIT (ANTI-OVERCORRECTION)**:
- Check `mov_history`. If the last **2 consecutive commands** are both `D1` or both `D2`, you **MUST STOP** issuing lateral slides.
- **It is STRICTLY FORBIDDEN to emit more than 2 consecutive D1 or D2 commands.**
- If you hit this limit: switch to `A1` (advance) or a micro-rotation (`B1`/`C1`) to break the pattern. Re-evaluate after moving forward.

**PRIORITY 5: CENTERED, PERPENDICULAR AND CLOSE -- ADVANCE TO CROSS**:
- **ALL THREE conditions must be TRUE simultaneously:**
  1. Door is centered in the frame (open void in the middle). `door_position` = `center`.
  2. Perpendicularity confirmed: you CANNOT see the inner depth of either doorframe (both are flat vertical lines). `door_angle` = `perpendicular`.
  3. You are close: doorframe sides fill a significant portion of the view.
- If all three are met -> return `A1` and **transition to `Go Through Door`**.
- **If any condition is NOT met, do NOT transition.** Stay in `Oriented Towards Door` and address the missing condition:
  - Not centered -> Priority 1 (rotate) or D1/D2 if close.
  - Not perpendicular -> Priority 2.5 (slide + rotate maneuver).
  - Not close enough -> Priority 2 (advance with A1).

**PRIORITY 6: DOOR LOST**:
- If the open door is no longer visible, switch back to `Search open Door`, only if you did not go through it. If you already crossed it, transition to `Position in the center of room` to reorient in the new room.

---

## MOVEMENT COMMANDS

- `A1`: move forward 10 cm -- **To approach when centered, or to cross when aligned at 0 degrees**
- `B1`: rotate 15 degrees left -- **To face a door on the left (far), micro-correct drift, or square up angle**
- `B2`: rotate 45 degrees left -- **To face a door far off to the left, or abort**
- `C1`: rotate 15 degrees right -- **To face a door on the right (far), micro-correct drift, or square up angle**
- `C2`: rotate 45 degrees right -- **To face a door far off to the right, or abort**
- `D1`: slide 5 cm left -- **ONLY for diagonal correction (Priority 2.5/3). Max 2 consecutive.**
- `D2`: slide 5 cm right -- **ONLY for diagonal correction (Priority 2.5/3). Max 2 consecutive.**

---

## OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Go Through Door`: **ONLY when ALL THREE conditions are confirmed**: (1) door centered, (2) `door_angle` = `perpendicular` (both frames are flat lines, no inner depth visible), (3) close enough that frames dominate the view. If ANY condition is uncertain, stay in `Oriented Towards Door`.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Door visible but needs centering, approach, or angle adjustment. **This is the default -- stay here until you are CERTAIN of all three crossing conditions.**
- `Search open Door`: Door is closed, is a cabinet, or is lost from view.
