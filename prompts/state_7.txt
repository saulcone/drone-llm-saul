## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Align the robot directly in front of an OPEN door without getting too close and losing visual context.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Strict Priority)

🚨 **CRITICAL ABORT (CLOSED DOORS)**:
- If you are facing a **CLOSED DOOR, CABINET, or CLOSET**, you are in the wrong state.
- **IMMEDIATELY** switch state to `Search open Door` and return `B2` or `C2` to look away. 
- **NEVER use `A1` against a closed surface.**

1️⃣ **ALIGNMENT LOCK (STRICT)**:
- If `"door_position"` evaluates to `"left"`, your `"movement"` MUST be `"B1"`.
- If `"door_position"` evaluates to `"right"`, your `"movement"` MUST be `"C1"`.
- 🛑 **RULE**: You are strictly FORBIDDEN from outputting `"A1"` if the door is off-center. Do not attempt to approach diagonally.

2️⃣ **LATERAL CORRECTION**:
- Use `D1`/`D2` ONLY if `"door_position"` is `"center"` but you are physically offset (parallel shift).

3️⃣ **FIELD OF VIEW PRESERVATION (DO NOT GET TOO CLOSE)**:
- Do **NOT** use `A1` if the door frame or walls are clipping out of the edges of the image (meaning you are extremely close and losing context).
- If you are fully centered and the door appears very close (occupying most of the view), you must transition to `Go Through Door`.

4️⃣ **FORWARD APPROACH**:
- Only use `"movement": "A1"` if `"door_position"` is EXACTLY `"center"`, the door is an OPEN VOID, and you still have good visual context (not glued to the frame).

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return **ONE** movement command from the list below:

### A. Forward movement:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm

### B. Left rotation:
- `B1`: rotate 15°
- `B2`: rotate 45° (Use ONLY for aborting)

### C. Right rotation:
- `C1`: rotate 15°
- `C2`: rotate 45° (Use ONLY for aborting)

### D. Lateral movement:
- `D1`: move 10 cm left  
- `D2`: move 10 cm right

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Go Through Door`: Open door is centered and close enough to cross.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Open door is visible but needs alignment or safe approach.
- `Search open Door`: Door is CLOSED, is a cabinet, or is lost from view.
