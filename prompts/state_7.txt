## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL
Align until the open door is **centered in the frame AND you are facing it perpendicularly (head-on)**. Do NOT enter at a diagonal angle.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Strict Priority)

🚨 **PRIORITY 0: ABORT CLOSED OBJECTS**:
- If the visible object is a **CLOSED door, cabinet, or closet**, it is NOT the target.
- Switch to `Search open Door` and rotate away (`B2` or `C2`).

🔍 **PRIORITY 1: MULTIPLE DOORS RESOLUTION**:
- If there are MULTIPLE open doors visible, you MUST select the correct one using the **topological map**.
- Look at the room connections in the map: identify which adjacent room matches your objective.
- Examine what's **visible through each door** (flooring, wall color, objects) to match it to the target room in the map.
- Once selected, treat only that door as the target and ignore all others completely.

🔁 **PRIORITY 1.5: ROTATION OSCILLATION DETECTION**:
- Check `mov_history`. If it contains **any alternation of `B1` and `C1`** (e.g., B1 then C1, or C1 then B1), you are trapped in a rotation loop.
- **This means you are NOT actually at a diagonal angle — you are laterally displaced from the door.** Stop rotating entirely.
- Identify which side the door is on and **slide toward it** (`D1` if door is left, `D2` if door is right) to reposition laterally in front of it.
- Do NOT attempt any B1/C1 rotation until after at least one D-slide step.

↔️ **PRIORITY 2: LATERAL CENTERING (DO THIS FIRST)**:
- **Before checking your rotation angle**, determine if the door opening is left, center, or right in the frame.
- **If door is to the LEFT** → slide left: `D1`. Keep repeating `D1` each step until centered.
- **If door is to the RIGHT** → slide right: `D2`. Keep repeating `D2` each step until centered.
- **If door is roughly CENTER** → proceed to Priority 3 to assess your angle.
- **Critical reasoning**: When the door is off to one side, you will naturally see the inner depth of the nearer doorframe — this is caused by your **lateral displacement**, NOT by a rotation angle. Do NOT attempt B1/C1 to fix it. Slide to center first, then assess angle.
- **Never rotate (B1/C1) when the door is off-center.** Lateral slides preserve your wall-facing angle; rotations corrupt it.

📐 **PRIORITY 3: PERPENDICULARITY CHECK (ONLY WHEN DOOR IS CENTERED)**:
- Only run this check once the door opening is roughly centered in the frame.
- **VISUAL CUE:** Look at the horizontal lines (ceiling/floor) inside the doorway. If the lines are slanted, or if you clearly see the deep "thickness" (inner depth) of one doorframe **while the door is centered**, you are at a true diagonal angle.
- **Rotation loop escape**: If `mov_history` shows **3 or more consecutive B1/C1 rotations of the same direction** and perpendicularity is still unclear, STOP rotating. Assume close enough and proceed to Priority 4.
- **ACTION:** Use a single small rotation to square up:
    - If you see the inner depth of the LEFT doorframe → rotate LEFT (`B1`).
    - If you see the inner depth of the RIGHT doorframe → rotate RIGHT (`C1`).
- **After any B1/C1 rotation**: re-evaluate BOTH lateral centering and perpendicularity from scratch before the next command.

✅ **PRIORITY 4: CENTERED & PERPENDICULAR — ADVANCE**:
- Door is in the center of the vision, you are facing it head-on, AND it is an open void → return `A1`.
- **Distance check before transitioning state**:
  - If the door appears **small or distant** (the doorframe edges are thin or partially outside the frame), keep state as `Oriented Towards Door` — you are still approaching, not yet crossing.
  - Only switch to `Go Through Door` when you are **close enough that both doorframe sides are clearly visible** and you are about to enter the opening.
- This is the ONLY condition under which `A1` is allowed.

🚪 **PRIORITY 5: DOOR LOST**:
- If the open door is no longer visible, switch back to `Search open Door`, only if you didn't go through it. If you already crossed it, transition to `Position in the center of room` to reorient in the new room.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

- `A1`: move forward 10 cm ← **ONLY when centered AND perpendicular**
- `B1`: rotate 15° left ← **ONLY to fix a diagonal angle**
- `C1`: rotate 15° right ← **ONLY to fix a diagonal angle**
- `D1`: slide 5 cm left ← **ONLY to center the door when facing it head-on**
- `D2`: slide 5 cm right ← **ONLY to center the door when facing it head-on**
- `B2` / `C2`: rotate 45° ← abort only (door at edge or lost)

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Go Through Door`: Door is centered, you are perpendicular, and `A1` will cross it perfectly straight.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Door visible but needs centering or angle adjustment.
- `Search open Door`: Door is closed, is a cabinet, or is lost from view.
