## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL
APPROACH the door by keeping it roughly CENTERED. If door drifts left/right, rotate to re-center before advancing.

**Transition to `Go Through Door`**: When door occupies >40% of frame width.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Strict Priority)

🔁 **RULE 0: ANTI-OSCILLATION (CHECK FIRST)**:
- **Look at `mov_history`** (last 4-6 movements)
- **If you see alternating B1↔C1 pattern** (e.g., C1,C1,B1,B1,C1,B1):
  - You are OSCILLATING because 15° rotations are too big
  - **Switch to MICRO-ROTATIONS: B0/C0 (5°) instead of B1/C1**
  - This will let you fine-tune without overshooting

🎯 **RULE 1: CENTER THE DOOR**:
| door_position | No oscillation | Oscillating (B1↔C1 in history) |
|---------------|----------------|--------------------------------|
| `left` | `B1` (15° left) | `B0` (5° micro-left) |
| `right` | `C1` (15° right) | `C0` (5° micro-right) |
| `center` | `A1` (advance) | `A1` (advance) |

🚶 **RULE 2: ADVANCE WHEN CENTERED**:
- When `door_position` = `center` → Advance with `A1`

🔄 **RULE 3: TRANSITION TO CROSSING**:
- **When**: Door occupies >40% of frame width (you are CLOSE)
- **Action**: Change state to `Go Through Door`

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

| Situation | Command |
|-----------|---------|
| Door clearly LEFT (no oscillation) | `B1` (15°) |
| Door clearly RIGHT (no oscillation) | `C1` (15°) |
| Door slightly LEFT (or oscillating) | `B0` (5°) |
| Door slightly RIGHT (or oscillating) | `C0` (5°) |
| Door CENTERED | `A1` |

---

## ⚠️ OSCILLATION DETECTION

**How to detect oscillation:**
- Look at the last 4-6 commands in `mov_history`
- Pattern like: `B1, C1, B1, C1` or `C1, C1, B1, B1, C1` = OSCILLATING
- **Fix**: Use micro-rotations (B0/C0) instead of large rotations (B1/C1)

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Oriented Towards Door`: Door visible, keep approaching.
- `Go Through Door`: Door occupies >40% of frame — ready for precision crossing.
- `Search open Door`: Door lost from view.
