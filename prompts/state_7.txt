## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL
Align until the open door is **centered in the frame AND you are facing it perpendicularly (head-on)**. Do NOT enter at a diagonal angle.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Strict Priority)

🚨 **PRIORITY 0: ABORT CLOSED OBJECTS**:
- If the visible object is a **CLOSED door, cabinet, or closet**, it is NOT the target.
- Switch to `Search open Door` and rotate away (`B2` or `C2`).

🔍 **PRIORITY 1: MULTIPLE DOORS RESOLUTION**:
- If there are MULTIPLE open doors visible, you MUST choose the one that leads to your target destination based on your user query.
- Ignore the wrong door completely.

🔁 **PRIORITY 1.5: ANTI-OSCILLATION**:
- Check `mov_history`. If the last **3+ commands alternate `D1` and `D2`**, you are oscillating and already centered. **Return `A1` immediately.**

↔️ **PRIORITY 2: CENTERING (MANDATORY FIRST STEP)**:
- Divide the frame into three equal vertical thirds: left / center / right.
- **If the target door opening is in the LEFT third** → slide left: `D1`
- **If the target door opening is in the RIGHT third** → slide right: `D2`
- **CRITICAL:** Do NOT attempt to fix a diagonal angle until the door is in the CENTER third. You must center it first so you don't lose sight of it when rotating.

📐 **PRIORITY 3: PERPENDICULARITY CHECK (ONLY WHEN CENTERED)**:
- Once the target door is in the CENTER third, check if you are facing it perfectly straight-on (head-on).
- **VISUAL CUE:** Look at the horizontal lines (the top of the doorframe, floor lines, or wall edges). If they are slanted/skewed, or if you can see the deep inside thickness of one of the doorframes, you are looking at a diagonal angle.
- **ACTION:** If you are diagonal, use a small rotation (`B1` to rotate left, `C1` to rotate right) to "square up" and face the wall flatly. 
- **PROHIBITED:** Never use `B1` or `C1` to move the door left or right across the screen. Rotations are ONLY for fixing the diagonal angle once the door is already centered.

✅ **PRIORITY 4: CENTERED & PERPENDICULAR — ADVANCE**:
- Door is in the center third, you are facing it head-on, AND it is an open void → return `A1`.
- This is the ONLY condition under which `A1` is allowed.

🚪 **PRIORITY 5: DOOR LOST**:
- If the open door is no longer visible, switch back to `Search open Door`.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

- `A1`: move forward 10 cm ← **ONLY when centered AND perpendicular**
- `B1`: rotate 15° left ← **ONLY to fix a diagonal angle**
- `C1`: rotate 15° right ← **ONLY to fix a diagonal angle**
- `D1`: slide 5 cm left ← **ONLY to center the door when facing it head-on**
- `D2`: slide 5 cm right ← **ONLY to center the door when facing it head-on**
- `B2` / `C2`: rotate 45° ← abort only (door at edge or lost)

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Go Through Door`: Door is centered, you are perpendicular, and `A1` will cross it perfectly straight.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Door visible but needs centering or angle adjustment.
- `Search open Door`: Door is closed, is a cabinet, or is lost from view.
