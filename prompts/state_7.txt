## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Align the robot directly in front of an OPEN door.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Strict Priority)

🚨 **CRITICAL ABORT (CLOSED DOORS)**:
- If you are facing a **CLOSED DOOR, CABINET, or CLOSET**, you are in the wrong state.
- **IMMEDIATELY** switch state to `Search open Door` and return `B2` or `C2` to look away. 
- **NEVER use `A1` against a closed surface.**

1️⃣ **ROTATION PRIORITY (Center the Target First)**:
- If the OPEN door is "left": **You MUST return `B1`**. Do NOT move forward (`A1`).
- If the OPEN door is "right": **You MUST return `C1`**. Do NOT move forward (`A1`).

2️⃣ **LATERAL CORRECTION**:
- Use `D1`/`D2` ONLY if the door is "center" but you are physically offset (parallel shift), and you are close.

3️⃣ **FORWARD APPROACH**:
- Only use `A1` if `door_position` is "center" AND the door is clearly an OPEN VOID.
- 🛑 **NUCLEAR RULE**: If you use the words "closed", "cabinet", "closet", or "knobs" anywhere in your `description`, your `movement` CANNOT be `A1`. You MUST abort using `B2` or `C2` and switch to `Search open Door`.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return **ONE** movement command from the list below:

### A. Forward movement:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm

### B. Left rotation:
- `B1`: rotate 15°
- `B2`: rotate 45° (Use ONLY for aborting)

### C. Right rotation:
- `C1`: rotate 15°
- `C2`: rotate 45° (Use ONLY for aborting)

### D. Lateral movement:
- `D1`: move 10 cm left  
- `D2`: move 10 cm right

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

Choose the next FSM state based on door position and visual characteristics:

- `Go Through Door`: Open door is centered and close.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Open door is visible but needs alignment.
- `Search open Door`: Door is CLOSED, is a cabinet, or is lost from view.
