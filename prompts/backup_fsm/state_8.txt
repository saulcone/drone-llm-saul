## State: **Go Through Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
You are CLOSE to the door (>40% of frame). Cross through safely using L-PATTERN with EXTRA CAUTION:
1. **CENTER** the door opening in the middle of your view
2. **VERIFY MARGINS** — need clear space on BOTH sides for your body
3. **ADVANCE** through when properly aligned

---

## ⚠️ CRITICAL: YOUR BODY IS WIDER THAN YOUR CAMERA!

```

        ┌───────────┐              ┌─────────────────┐
```

**When crossing a door, you need EXTRA clearance!**
- The door opening should be in the CENTER of your view
- You need to see OPEN SPACE (floor, next room) on BOTH left and right edges
- If you see wall on either edge → you WILL hit it with your body

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES — SAFE CROSSING

🎯 **RULE 1: CENTER THE DOOR (Strafe)**
| door_position | Action |
|---------------|--------|
| `left` | `D1` (strafe LEFT towards door) |
| `right` | `D2` (strafe RIGHT towards door) |
| `center` | → Go to RULE 2 |

📐 **RULE 2: MARGIN CHECK — Can your body fit?**
- **Look at the LEFT 20% of your view**: Do you see WALL or OPEN SPACE?
- **Look at the RIGHT 20% of your view**: Do you see WALL or OPEN SPACE?
- **Decision**:
  - LEFT edge shows wall → `D2` (strafe right for more margin)
  - RIGHT edge shows wall → `D1` (strafe left for more margin)
  - BOTH edges show open space/floor → `A1` (safe to advance)

✅ **RULE 3: CROSS**
- **Condition**: Door centered + clear margins on both sides
- **Action**: `A1` to advance through
- Keep advancing until door frame is BEHIND you

🔁 **RULE 4: ANTI-OSCILLATION**
- If last 4 commands alternate D1↔D2 → just `A1` to break the loop

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

| Situation | Command |
|-----------|---------|
| Door on LEFT | `D1` (strafe left towards door) |
| Door on RIGHT | `D2` (strafe right towards door) |
| Door centered BUT wall visible on LEFT edge | `D2` (need more right margin) |
| Door centered BUT wall visible on RIGHT edge | `D1` (need more left margin) |
| Door centered + BOTH edges show open space | `A1` (safe to cross!) |

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Go Through Door`: Still crossing.
- `Position in the center of room`: Door frame is BEHIND you — you have crossed!
- `Search open Door`: Lost the door.
