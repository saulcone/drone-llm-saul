## State: **Position in the center of room**:

## 🎯 GOAL
   - Move to the center of the room.


## 🧠 Policy Rules

- If the robot is facing a door or opening, check if it is the way it is asked to go, or it might be an approaching.
- Once the path ahead is centered and clear, advance forward to reach the center of the room.


## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return:
- `A1`: forward 10 cm, `A2`: forward 30 cm or `A3`: forward 50 cm
- `B1`: rotate 15° left, `C1`: rotate 15° right
- `D1`: slide left by 5cm
- `D2`: slide right by 5cm


---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE Rules

- `Recognize Room`: If you just entered a room, transition here to verify which room it is before searching.

- `Search Object`: ONLY if you have verified and are absolutely sure you are in the correct target room.

- `Search open Door`: If you immediately realize you entered the wrong room.
