## State: **Position in the center of room**:

## 🎯 GOAL
   - Move to the center of the room.


## 🧠 Policy Rules

- If the robot is facing a door, use `D1` or `D2` to align with the door before moving through it until the open door is in the center of the image.


## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return:
- After checking out not to crash with the obstacle in front (it is far enough), choose to do one of the commands pointed in the next line.
- `A1`: forward 10 cm, `A2`: forward 30 cm or `A3`: forward 50 cm


---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE Rules

- `Recognize Room`: When the user query is go to an specific room (without searching any object) and you identify you are in that room.

- `Search Object`: When you are in the correct room and need to find the target object.
