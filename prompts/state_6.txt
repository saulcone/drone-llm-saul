## State: **Search open Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Systematically sweep the environment to detect an open door. Follow a structured **rotate → advance → rotate** cycle. Never spin in place indefinitely.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Priority Ordered)

✅ **RULE 0: ALREADY IN TARGET ROOM (CHECK FIRST)**:
- Compare the current room against the user query target room.
- If visual cues confirm you are **already inside the correct target room**, do **NOT** search for a door.
- **Immediately transition to `Search Object`** with movement `E`.

🚨 **RULE 1: ABSOLUTE SAFETY & SWEEPING**:
- Search ONLY for **OPEN DOORS**. Ignore closed doors, cabinets, and walls.
- Rotate in the same direction to sweep effectively.
- If you complete a large rotation without finding anything, use `A1` or `A2` to move to a new vantage point before resuming rotations.
- **WRONG ROOM ESCAPE**: If you just realized you are in the wrong room, execute a large rotation (`B3` or `C3`) to quickly turn around and locate the exit.

🚪 **RULE 2: OPEN DOOR DETECTED (TRIGGER)**:
- If you see an open door or open void leading to another room that could plausibly lead to your objective:
- **ACTION**: You MUST immediately return movement command `E` and change the state to `Oriented Towards Door`.
- **CRITICAL**: Do NOT attempt to align, rotate, or slide towards the door in this state. Just emit `E`. Alignment happens in the next state.

🚶 **RULE 3: CLEAR OPEN SPACE**:
- If no door is visible but the path ahead is a large open space with no obstacles, use `A1` to move efficiently.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS
Only return ONE command:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm
- `A2`: move forward 30 cm
- `B2`/`B3`: rotate left
- `C2`/`C3`: rotate right
- `E`: transition command — to `Oriented Towards Door` (open door found) OR to `Search Object` (already in target room)

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES
- `Search Object`: Already in the target room.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: An open door is detected.
- `Search open Door`: Still looking, sweeping, or moving.
