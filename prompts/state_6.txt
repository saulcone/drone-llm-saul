## State: **Search open Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Systematically sweep the environment to detect an open door. Follow a structured **rotate → advance → rotate** cycle. Never spin in place indefinitely.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS
Only return ONE command:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm (Use to explore and change location)
- `A2`: move forward 30 cm (use when the path ahead is clearly open)
- `B2`/`B3`: rotate left
- `C2`/`C3`: rotate right
- `D1`: slide 5cm left
- `D2`: slide 5cm right
- `E`: transition command — to `Oriented Towards Door` (open door found) OR to `Search Object` (already in target room)

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Priority Ordered)

✅ **PRIORITY 0: ALREADY IN TARGET ROOM (CHECK FIRST)**:
- Before anything else, compare the room you are currently in against the room mentioned in the user query (e.g., "find the sink in the bathroom" → target room is "bathroom").
- If the visual cues (floor pattern, objects, room features) confirm you are **already inside the correct target room**, do **NOT** search for a door.
- **Immediately transition to `Search Object`** with movement `E`.
- This overrides all navigation rules below. You are where you need to be — start looking for the object.

🚨 **RULE 1: ABSOLUTE SAFETY — CABINETS / WALLS / CORNERS**:
- Just search for **OPEN DOORS**, any path non-suitable should be discarded and avoided.
- To do a effective search, rotate in the same direction.
- If made a 360 degrees rotation, stop, and consider doing a different move (slide, moving forward).

⚠️ **WRONG ROOM ESCAPE**: If you just realized you are in the wrong room, your immediate priority is to find the door you just came through. Execute a large rotation (B3 or C3, 90 degrees) to quickly turn around and locate the exit. 


3️⃣ **RULE 2 (OPEN DOOR DETECTED)**:
- If you see an open door or open void leading to another room, transition to `Oriented Towards Door` (`E`) **ONLY IF IT COULD PLAUSIBLY LEAD TO THE OBJECTIVE**.
- Align with the door to approach it in a perpendicular way, by rotating if not perpendicularly aligned to the door, and if it is by sliding (`D1` or `D2`). Change to **Oriented Towards Door**.


5️⃣ **RULE 3 (CLEAR OPEN SPACE)**:
- If the path ahead is a large open space with no obstacles, use `A1` (10 cm) to move efficiently toward the center of the room.

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES
- `Search Object`: You have confirmed you are **already in the correct target room** — stop navigating, start searching for the object.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: An open door is detected and selected as a candidate.
- `Search open Door`: Still looking, moving, or dodging a closed door/cabinet.
