## State: **Search open Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Systematically explore the environment to detect an open door.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS
Only return ONE command:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm
- `B1`/`B2`/`B3`: rotate left
- `C1`/`C2`/`C3`: rotate right
- `E`: transition to 'Oriented Towards Door'

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (priority ordered)

🚨 **ZERO PRIORITY (CABINET/CLOSED DOOR AVOIDANCE)**:
If the object directly in front of you is CLOSED (a closed door, a cabinet, a closet), treat it as a SOLID WALL. It is NOT an open door. Rotate (`B2` or `C2`) to look away from it.

1️⃣ **FIRST PRIORITY (ALIGN BEFORE MOVING)**:
If an **open door** is visible:
- If `door_position` is "left" or "right", switch to `Oriented Towards Door` (`E`). **NEVER move forward (`A1`) if the open door is on the side.**
- If `door_position` is "center", you may move forward (`A1`).

2️⃣ **SECOND PRIORITY (TOPOLOGY)**:
If you confidently identify the room beyond the open door as the goal room or a connected room, switch to `Oriented Towards Door` (`E`).

3️⃣ **THIRD PRIORITY (SEARCH)**:
If no open door is visible:
- Rotate (`B2`/`C2`) to explore. 
- If stuck facing a wall/closet for multiple steps, use `B3` or `C3` to turn away drastically.

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Only if an OPEN door is visible. (Requires movement `E`).
- `Search open Door`: If no open door is seen, or if facing a CLOSED door/cabinet.
