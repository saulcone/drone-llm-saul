## State: **Search open Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Systematically explore the environment to detect an open door. You must physically move through the room, not just rotate in place.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS
Only return ONE command:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm (Use to explore and change location)
- `B2`/`B3`: rotate left
- `C2`/`C3`: rotate right
- `E`: transition to 'Oriented Towards Door'

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (priority ordered)

🚨 **ZERO PRIORITY (IGNORE CLOSED OBJECTS)**:
If the object directly in front is CLOSED (closed door, cabinet, closet), it is a WALL. 
Rotate (`B2` or `C2`) to look away.

⚠️ **FALSE DOOR WARNING (CHIMNEY/FIREPLACE)**:
If a gap or void is located directly adjacent to a fireplace or chimney, it is **NOT** a passable doorway — it is an architectural recess. Be very skeptical. Do NOT select it as a door candidate. Keep searching.

1️⃣ **FIRST PRIORITY (VISUAL PREDICTION)**:
If you see an **open door**:
- If it looks like the target room OR you are unsure, switch to `Oriented Towards Door` (`E`).
- NEVER approach diagonally with `A1`.

2️⃣ **SECOND PRIORITY (ANTI-LOOP MOVEMENT - STRICT)**:
- **CRITICAL**: You must NOT get stuck in infinite rotation loops.
- If your `previous_movement` was a rotation command (`B1`, `B2`, `B3`, `C1`, `C2`, `C3`) AND no open door is visible, you **MUST** return `A1` to move forward and change your physical location.
- DO NOT rotate twice in a row unless you are physically blocked by a wall directly in front of you (less than 50cm away).

3️⃣ **THIRD PRIORITY (STANDARD SEARCH)**:
- If your `previous_movement` was a forward movement (`A1`, `A2`) and no door is visible, rotate (`B2` or `C2`) to scan the new area.
- This creates an explore pattern: Rotate -> Move Forward -> Rotate -> Move Forward.

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES
- `Oriented Towards Door`: An open door is detected and selected as a candidate.
- `Search open Door`: Still looking, moving, or dodging a closed door/cabinet.
