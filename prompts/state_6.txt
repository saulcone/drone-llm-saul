## State: **Search open Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Systematically explore the environment to detect an open door. You must physically move through the room, not just rotate in place.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS
Only return ONE command:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm (Use to explore and change location)
- `A2`: move forward 30 cm (use when the path ahead is clearly open)
- `B2`/`B3`: rotate left
- `C2`/`C3`: rotate right
- `E`: transition to 'Oriented Towards Door'

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Priority Ordered)

🚨 PRIORITY 0: ABSOLUTE SAFETY — CABINETS / WALLS / CORNERS:
- If the object directly in front is a CLOSED DOOR, CABINET, CLOSET, or WALL, you are FORBIDDEN from using `A1`.
- CRITICAL: Always rotate in the SAME direction as your `previous_movement` (e.g., if previous was C, use C; if B, use B) to avoid infinite oscillation loops. NEVER reverse your rotation immediately.
⚠️ WRONG ROOM ESCAPE: If you just realized you are in the wrong room, your immediate priority is to find the door you just came through. Execute a large rotation (B3 or C3, 90 degrees) to quickly turn around and locate the exit. Do not waste time with small 15-degree rotations.

1️⃣ **FIRST PRIORITY (OPEN DOOR DETECTED)**:
- If you see an open door leading to another room, transition to `Oriented Towards Door` (`E`) **ONLY IF IT'S POSSIBLE IT LEADS TO THE OBJECTIVE**.
- **Do NOT approach it diagonally with `A1`**. Let the Oriented state handle the approach.

2️⃣ **SECOND PRIORITY (EXPLORATION / ANTI-LOOP)**:
- If `previous_movement` was a rotation AND no open door is visible AND the **path ahead is OPEN and CLEAR** (no wall, no cabinet, no closed door close in front): return `A1` to move forward and change vantage point.
- If the path is NOT clear, continue rotating — this does NOT count as a loop.

3️⃣ THIRD PRIORITY (MOVE TO OPEN SPACE):

    If the path ahead is a large open space, continue moving forward (A1) to reach the center of the room.

    DO NOT rotate if you have a clear, deep path ahead. You must chain multiple A1 movements until you get closer to the center or detect an obstacle.

    Only rotate (B2 or C2) if you have reached a wall, a corner, or the end of the open space.
---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES
- `Oriented Towards Door`: An open door is detected and selected as a candidate.
- `Search open Door`: Still looking, moving, or dodging a closed door/cabinet.
