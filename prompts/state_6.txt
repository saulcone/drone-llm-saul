## State: **Search open Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Systematically explore the environment to detect an open door. You must physically move through the room, not just rotate in place.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS
Only return ONE command:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm (Use to explore and change location)
- `B2`/`B3`: rotate left
- `C2`/`C3`: rotate right
- `E`: transition to 'Oriented Towards Door'

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (priority ordered)

🚨 **ZERO PRIORITY — WALL / COLLISION AVOIDANCE (CHECK FIRST)**:
- If you see a **wall, surface, or solid obstacle very close** in front of you (filling most of the frame), **DO NOT use `A1`**. You will collide.
- Instead, rotate away: `B2` or `C2`.
- Closed doors, cabinets, and closets count as walls — rotate to look away.

⚠️ **FALSE DOOR WARNING (CHIMNEY/FIREPLACE)**:
If a gap or void is located directly adjacent to a fireplace or chimney, it is **NOT** a passable doorway — it is an architectural recess. Be very skeptical. Do NOT select it as a door candidate. Keep searching.

1️⃣ **FIRST PRIORITY (OPEN DOOR DETECTED)**:
If you see an **open door**:
- If it looks like the target room OR you are unsure, switch to `Oriented Towards Door` (`E`).
- NEVER approach diagonally with `A1`.

2️⃣ **SECOND PRIORITY — ANTI-LOOP MOVEMENT**:
- If `previous_movement` was a rotation AND no open door is visible AND the space ahead is **open/clear** (no wall close in front): return `A1` to move forward.
- **EXCEPTION**: If a wall or surface fills most of the frame directly ahead, do NOT use `A1`. Rotate again — this does NOT count as a loop.

3️⃣ **THIRD PRIORITY — ROTATION DIRECTION (ALTERNATE!)**:
- If `previous_movement` was a forward movement (`A1`) and no door is visible, rotate to scan.
- **You MUST alternate rotation direction**. Check `mov_history`:
  - If your recent rotations were mostly to the **right** (`C2`, `C3`), rotate **left** (`B2`).
  - If your recent rotations were mostly to the **left** (`B2`, `B3`), rotate **right** (`C2`).
  - This prevents spiraling into a corner. Cover both sides of the room.

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES
- `Oriented Towards Door`: An open door is detected and selected as a candidate.
- `Search open Door`: Still looking, moving, or dodging a closed door/cabinet.
