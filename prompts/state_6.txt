## State: **Search open Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Systematically explore the environment to detect an open door. You must physically move through the room, not just rotate in place.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS
Only return ONE command:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm (Use to explore and change location)
- `B2`/`B3`: rotate left
- `C2`/`C3`: rotate right
- `E`: transition to 'Oriented Towards Door'

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (priority ordered)

🚨 **ZERO PRIORITY — STUCK IN CORNER ESCAPE (CHECK `mov_history` FIRST)**:
- Look at `mov_history`. If the last **3 or more commands are ALL rotations** (no `A1` among them), you are **stuck in a corner**.
- When stuck, **do NOT keep rotating with B2/C2**. You must escape:
  - Use `B3` or `C3` (90° rotation) to point in a completely new direction.
  - Pick the direction that is AWAY from the walls you have been seeing (if unsure, pick `B3`).
- After the big rotation, you MUST use `A1` on the next step to physically leave the corner, even if the space ahead is not perfectly clear. A wall far away (more than ~30cm) is safe enough.

1️⃣ **FIRST PRIORITY — WALL / COLLISION AVOIDANCE**:
- If a **wall or solid obstacle is extremely close** (filling >80% of the frame), do NOT use `A1`.
- Rotate away: `B2` or `C2`.
- Closed doors, cabinets, and closets count as walls — rotate to look away.
- **IMPORTANT**: A wall that is **far away** (you can see floor in front of you) is NOT a collision risk. You CAN use `A1` in that case.

⚠️ **FALSE DOOR WARNING (CHIMNEY/FIREPLACE)**:
If a gap or void is located directly adjacent to a fireplace or chimney, it is **NOT** a passable doorway — it is an architectural recess. Keep searching.

2️⃣ **SECOND PRIORITY (OPEN DOOR DETECTED)**:
If you see an **open door**:
- If it looks like the target room OR you are unsure, switch to `Oriented Towards Door` (`E`).
- NEVER approach diagonally with `A1`.

3️⃣ **THIRD PRIORITY — EXPLORE PATTERN**:
- After a rotation: if the space ahead is reasonably clear, use `A1` to move forward.
- After `A1`: if no door is visible, rotate to scan. Alternate direction based on `mov_history` (if recent rotations were right, go left, and vice versa).

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES
- `Oriented Towards Door`: An open door is detected and selected as a candidate.
- `Search open Door`: Still looking, moving, or dodging a closed door/cabinet.
