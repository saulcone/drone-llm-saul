## State: **Search open Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Systematically explore the environment to detect an open door. You must physically move through the room, not just rotate in place.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS
Only return ONE command:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm (Use to explore and change location)
- `B2`/`B3`: rotate left
- `C2`/`C3`: rotate right
- `E`: transition to 'Oriented Towards Door'

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Priority Ordered)

🚨 **PRIORITY 0: ABSOLUTE SAFETY — CABINETS / WALLS / CORNERS**:
- If the object directly in front is a **CLOSED DOOR, CABINET, CLOSET, or WALL**, you are **FORBIDDEN from using `A1`**.
- If you are **facing a corner** (walls visible on BOTH left and right sides of the frame simultaneously, or a narrow space where walls close in from both sides), you are **FORBIDDEN from using `A1`**.
- In corner situations, use a **large rotation** (`B3` or `C3`, 90°) to escape and point towards a more open area before attempting any forward movement.
- Return a rotation (`B2`, `C2`, `B3`, or `C3`) to look away.
- **Safety overrides ALL other rules including the anti-loop rule.**

⚠️ **FALSE DOOR WARNING (CHIMNEY/FIREPLACE)**:
If a gap or void is located directly adjacent to a fireplace or chimney, it is **NOT** a passable doorway. Keep searching.

1️⃣ **FIRST PRIORITY (OPEN DOOR DETECTED)**:
- If you see an open door leading to another room, transition to `Oriented Towards Door` (`E`).
- **Do NOT approach it diagonally with `A1`**. Let the Oriented state handle the approach.

2️⃣ **SECOND PRIORITY (EXPLORATION / ANTI-LOOP)**:
- If `previous_movement` was a rotation AND no open door is visible AND the **path ahead is OPEN and CLEAR** (no wall, no cabinet, no closed door close in front): return `A1` to move forward and change vantage point.
- If the path is NOT clear, continue rotating — this does NOT count as a loop.

3️⃣ **THIRD PRIORITY (STANDARD SEARCH)**:
- If `previous_movement` was `A1` and no door is visible, rotate (`B2` or `C2`) to scan the new area.
- Pattern: Rotate → Move Forward → Rotate → Move Forward.

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES
- `Oriented Towards Door`: An open door is detected and selected as a candidate.
- `Search open Door`: Still looking, moving, or dodging a closed door/cabinet.
