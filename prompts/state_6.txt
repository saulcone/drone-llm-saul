## State: **Search open Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Systematically explore the environment to detect an open door that leads to the target room.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS
Only return ONE command:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm (Use ONLY to unblock view or approach a candidate)
- `B1`/`B2`/`B3`: rotate left
- `C1`/`C2`/`C3`: rotate right
- `E`: transition to 'Oriented Towards Door'

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (priority ordered)

🚨 **ZERO PRIORITY (IGNORE CLOSED OBJECTS)**:
If the object directly in front is CLOSED (closed door, cabinet, closet), it is a WALL. 
Rotate (`B2` or `C2`) to look away.

1️⃣ **FIRST PRIORITY (VISUAL PREDICTION)**:
If you see an **open door**:
- **Analyze the room beyond**: Does it look like the target room? (e.g., if looking for 'bathroom', do you see tiles/white fixtures? If 'bedroom', a bed?).
- If it looks correct OR you are unsure, switch to `Oriented Towards Door` (`E`).
- **Safety**: If the door is on the side ("left"/"right"), use `E`. NEVER approach diagonally with `A1`.

2️⃣ **SECOND PRIORITY (UNBLOCK VIEW)**:
If you have been rotating for several steps and only see walls or closed furniture:
- You might be stuck in a "visual dead zone".
- **Perform `A1`** to change your vantage point and reveal new angles.
- Do NOT use `A1` if you are extremely close to a wall (less than 50cm).

3️⃣ **THIRD PRIORITY (STANDARD SEARCH)**:
If no open door is visible:
- Rotate (`B2` or `C2`) to scan 360°.
- Prefer large rotations (`B3`/`C3`) if the current view is completely uninteresting (blank wall).

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES
- `Oriented Towards Door`: An open door is detected and selected as a candidate.
- `Search open Door`: Still looking, or dodging a closed door/cabinet.
