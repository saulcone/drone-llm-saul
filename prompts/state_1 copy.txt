## State: **Position in the center of room**:

## 🎯 GOAL
   - Move until you are posed in the center of the room.


## 🧠 Policy Rules


- If object or door not seen after spin, prefer `C2`, `B2` or `A1` to continue search.
- Never use `D1` or `D2` unless in `"Reach object"` or `"Oriented towards door"`.
- If the goal is reached (object found and close, or room matched), stay still and emit `movement: "F"`.

- If the robot is facing a door, use `D1` or `D2` to align with the door before moving through it until the open door is in the center of the image.


## 🕹️ Movement Commands

Only return **ONE** command from the following options:

### A. Forward movement:
- `A1`: forward 10 cm
- `A2`: forward 25 cm
- `A3`: forward 50 cm

### B. Left rotation:
- `B1`: rotate 15°
- `B2`: rotate 45°
- `B3`: rotate 90°

### C. Right rotation:
- `C1`: rotate 15°
- `C2`: rotate 45°
- `C3`: rotate 90°

Return **the best** Possible output state

## 🔄 Possible output state:
- `Stay on Room`: When the user query is go to an specific room (without searching any object) and you identify you are in that room.
