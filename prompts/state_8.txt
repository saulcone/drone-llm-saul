## State: **Go Through Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Proceed through the centered OPEN door confidently without hugging the doorframes.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES

 "PRIORITY 1: DRIFT CORRECTION. If the door is significantly off-center or not in front (0 degrees) drifting towards a frame, use D1 or D2. However, if the door is only 'slightly' off-center but the path ahead is clearly open and safe to cross without hitting the frame, prioritize Forward Movement (A1) to avoid infinite lateral oscillation."

 "PRIORITY 2: ANTI-OSCILLATION (LATERAL). Check movement history. If the last movements alternate between D1 and D2 (e.g., D1 -> D2 -> D1), you are oscillating. The door is centered enough. You MUST ignore minor drifts and use A1 immediately to cross."
🚨 

**CRITICAL ABORT (BLINDNESS & CABINETS)**:
- If your view is suddenly completely blocked by a surface (you are glued to a wall), OR if you face a **CLOSED DOOR, CABINET, or CLOSET**:
  - **YOU MUST THINK ABOUT IF IT ISN'T A DOORWAY**. Switch state to `Search open Door`.
  -**IF IT IS THE DOORWAY** Check if it is near enough to worry, if it is not, go straight `A1`, if it is, go to Drif Correction (lateral).


- **Drift Correction (lateral)**:
  - If you are moving through the door but drifting towards one side frame, slide away: `D1` (drift right → slide left) or `D2` (drift left → slide right).
  - Do NOT rotate to correct drift — you would lose the door in your view.

- **Forward Movement**:
  - Use `A1` if the door is clearly an **open void**, fully visible, and you can see the room ahead, and it is likely to be the one you are looking for or the one that can contain the object you are looking for.

- **Refine Alignment**:
  - If the open door becomes very uncentered (more than half the frame), switch to `Oriented Towards Door` to recenter before proceeding.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm (ONLY IF PATH IS CLEAR AND OPEN)
- `D1`: slide 10 cm left (to correct rightward drift while keeping camera on door)
- `D2`: slide 10 cm right (to correct leftward drift while keeping camera on door)
- `B2`, `C2`: rotate 45° — use ONLY to abort when path is blocked

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES
- `Go Through Door`: Door is open, centered, and safe to cross.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Door is open but you drifted off-center.
- `Position in the center of room`: Threshold crossed successfully.
- `Search open Door`: The path is BLOCKED, closed, or you are completely glued to a frame (ABORT).
