## State: **Go Through Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Proceed through the OPEN door. **CRITICAL: NEVER collide with the doorframes.** You should already be aligned at ~0° from the `Oriented Towards Door` state. Your job now is to advance straight through with minimal corrections.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Strict Evaluation Order)

🛑 **RULE 1: VISIBILITY SAFETY CHECK (ALWAYS FIRST)**:
- **Condition**: If the image is blurry, completely uniform, or you cannot identify any doorframe or open void at all (camera clipping into a wall).
- **Action**: `A1` is STRICTLY FORBIDDEN. You MUST rotate away immediately to free the camera and switch state to `Search open Door`.

🔁 **RULE 2: ANTI-OSCILLATION (TRY TO ADVANCE)**:
- **Condition**: Inspect the **last 4 commands** in `mov_history`. If they contain **both `D1` and `D2`** (in any order), you are in a fatal overcorrection loop.
- **Action**: Return `A1` immediately to break the loop. Do NOT apply Rule 4 evasion.

🚪 **RULE 3: CRITICAL ABORT (CLOSED OBJECTS)**:
- **Condition**: If you face a **CLOSED DOOR, CABINET, or CLOSET** dead ahead.
- **Action**: Switch state to `Search open Door` and rotate away (`B2` or `C2`).

🚨 **RULE 4: CRITICAL EVASION (EXTREME COLLISION THRESHOLD)**:
- **Condition**: Only react in genuinely extreme situations. A single doorframe occupying 20–50% of the view is NORMAL for narrow doorways.
- **Extreme Threshold**: ONE doorframe occupies **>60% of the horizontal width** (or ~50% with obvious diagonal crash trajectory), AND the open void is pushed to the far opposite edge.
- **Action**:
  - **LEFT** frame is extreme -> slide RIGHT: `D2`.
  - **RIGHT** frame is extreme -> slide LEFT: `D1`.
- **Constraint**: After ONE evasive slide, your next step SHOULD be `A1`. Do not chain lateral slides. **CRITICAL: NEVER rotate (`B`/`C` commands) inside the doorway.**

✅ **RULE 5: SAFE FORWARD MOVEMENT (DEFAULT ACTION)**:
- **Condition**: The open void is visible ahead and neither frame triggers the extreme >60% threshold. (If BOTH frames are visible, you are centered).
- **Action**: Return `A1`.
- **Constraint**: DO NOT use `D1`/`D2` for "drift correction" or fine adjustments while inside the doorway. Trust your previous alignment. Small visual asymmetries are just perspective distortions. Advance blindly.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

- `A1`: move forward 10 cm — **DEFAULT command. Use this unless extreme evasion is needed.**
- `D1`: slide 5 cm left — **ONLY for extreme evasion (Rule 4). Never for drift correction.**
- `D2`: slide 5 cm right — **ONLY for extreme evasion (Rule 4). Never for drift correction.**
- `B2`, `C2`: rotate 45° — **ONLY for Rule 1 (Clipping) or Rule 3 (Abort).**

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Go Through Door`: Still crossing the door or evading an extreme collision.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Door is open but you lost alignment completely (visibility lost).
- `Position in the center of room`: Threshold crossed successfully (you are inside the new room).
- `Search open Door`: Path is blocked by a CLOSED object (ABORT).
