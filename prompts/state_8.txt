## State: **Go Through Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Proceed through the OPEN door. **CRITICAL: NEVER collide with the doorframes.** Anticipate collisions and slide laterally before they happen.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Strict Priority)

� **PRIORITY 0: ANTI-OSCILLATION (FORCE ADVANCE — CHECK FIRST)**:
- Before anything else, inspect `mov_history`. If it contains **any alternation of `D1` and `D2`** (even a single D1 followed by D2, or D2 followed by D1), you are in an overcorrection loop.
- **CRITICAL**: Return `A1` immediately, regardless of how the frames look. Lateral corrections are making things worse.
- **DO NOT apply Priority 1 collision avoidance if oscillation is detected.**

🚨 **PRIORITY 1: PROACTIVE COLLISION AVOIDANCE (ONLY FOR EXTREME CASES)**:
- A collision is a FATAL ERROR, but **only react when truly extreme**.
- **"Dangerously close" means**: A doorframe or wall is so close that it **fills or nearly fills one entire side of the image** (roughly >50% of the horizontal width), AND the open void is pushed to the far opposite side. This is a genuine imminent collision.
- A frame that is merely "visible", "prominent", or takes up 20–40% of the view is **NORMAL** for a narrow door. Do NOT trigger Priority 1 for this.
- **NARROW DOOR / BOTH FRAMES VISIBLE**: If you can see BOTH a left and a right doorframe with an open void between them — even if both feel close — YOU ARE CENTERED. **Do NOT slide.** Proceed with `A1`.
- **EQUALLY DISTANT FRAMES**: If both doorframes are arguably equidistant from the center of the image (the open void sits roughly in the middle), the robot is already centered. **Do NOT slide.** Return `A1` immediately.
- If and only if ONE frame meets the extreme >50% threshold and the other is barely or not visible:
  - If the **LEFT** frame is extremely close → slide RIGHT: `D2`.
  - If the **RIGHT** frame is extremely close → slide LEFT: `D1`.
- Keep the state as `Go Through Door`. **CRITICAL: Do NOT rotate (`B2`/`C2`).**

🚨 **PRIORITY 2: CRITICAL ABORT (ONLY CLOSED OBJECTS)**:
- ONLY abort if you face a **CLOSED DOOR, CABINET, or CLOSET** dead ahead.
- Switch state to `Search open Door` and rotate away (`B2` or `C2`).

↔️ **PRIORITY 3: DRIFT CORRECTION**:
- If the open void is visibly off-center (clearly shifted left or right) and you are NOT oscillating and NOT in danger, use ONE `D1` or `D2` to correct. Do not chain multiple corrections without re-evaluating.

✅ **PRIORITY 4: SAFE FORWARD MOVEMENT**:
- ONLY if the path is a clearly centered open void, use `A1` to cross.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm (Use to break oscillation, or if centered and safe)
- `D1`: slide 5 cm left (to avoid right frame or correct rightward drift)
- `D2`: slide 5 cm right (to avoid left frame or correct leftward drift)
- `B2`, `C2`: rotate 45° (ONLY for Priority 1 Abort)

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES
- `Go Through Door`: Still crossing the door, correcting drift, or evading a frame.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Door is open but you lost alignment completely.
- `Position in the center of room`: Threshold crossed successfully (you are inside the new room).
- `Search open Door`: Path is blocked by a CLOSED object (ABORT).
