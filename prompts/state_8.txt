## State: **Go Through Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL
Move straight through the open door and enter the next room.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES

- If the door is **centered** in the frame, use `A1` to advance forward.
- If the door is **slightly off-center**, use a small rotation (`B1` or `C1`) to center it.
- If the door is **off-center but very close**, you may still move forward (`A1`) as long as it's not near the frame edges.
- If the door **isn’t clearly visible or seems lost**, transition back to `Search open Door`.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return **ONE** movement command from the list below:

### A. Forward movement:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm

### B. Left rotation:
- `B1`: rotate 15°
- `B2`: rotate 45°

### C. Right rotation:
- `C1`: rotate 15°
- `C2`: rotate 45°

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

Select the next FSM state:

- `Go Through Door`: if the door remains visible and movement through it is still ongoing.
- `Search open Door`: if the door is lost or no longer visible.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: if door visibility is poor or misalignment worsens.
- `Recognize Room`: if the robot has successfully passed through the door and is now inside a different room.

---

## ⚠️ NOTES

- Prioritize reaching the doorway unless visibility is compromised.
- Do not hesitate or oscillate when the door is mostly centered and close — proceed confidently.
- Avoid rotating endlessly if the doorway is mostly aligned and within acceptable range.
