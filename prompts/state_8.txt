## State: **Go Through Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Proceed through the OPEN door. **CRITICAL: NEVER collide with the doorframes.** Anticipate collisions and slide laterally before they happen.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Strict Priority)

🚨 **PRIORITY 0: PROACTIVE COLLISION AVOIDANCE (NEVER HIT THE FRAME)**:
- A collision is a FATAL ERROR. You must anticipate it.
- If ONE wall or doorframe is getting dangerously close, taking up a much larger portion of the view than the other side, **DO NOT move forward (`A1`)**.
- You must slide laterally AWAY from the threatening frame:
  - If the **LEFT** frame is dangerously close → slide RIGHT: `D2`.
  - If the **RIGHT** frame is dangerously close → slide LEFT: `D1`.
- **NARROW DOOR EXCEPTION:** If BOTH the left and right frames are visible, close, and look roughly symmetrical (the void is in the middle), YOU ARE PERFECTLY CENTERED. Do NOT slide. Ignore Priority 0 and proceed to cross (`A1`).
- Keep the state as `Go Through Door`. **CRITICAL: Do NOT rotate (`B2`/`C2`).**

🚨 **PRIORITY 1: CRITICAL ABORT (ONLY CLOSED OBJECTS)**:
- ONLY abort if you face a **CLOSED DOOR, CABINET, or CLOSET** dead ahead.
- Switch state to `Search open Door` and rotate away (`B2` or `C2`).

🔁 **PRIORITY 2: ANTI-OSCILLATION (FORCE ADVANCE)**:
- Look closely at your `mov_history`. If your recent history contains a mix of BOTH `D1` and `D2` (for example, you used D2, D2, and then D1, D1), you are trapped in an overcorrection loop.
- This means you are already centered enough. **CRITICAL**: Stop sliding laterally. Return `A1` immediately to break the loop and move forward.

↔️ **PRIORITY 3: DRIFT CORRECTION**:
- If the open void is visibly off-center and you are NOT oscillating, use `D1` (slide left) or `D2` (slide right) to perfectly center it before continuing forward.

✅ **PRIORITY 4: SAFE FORWARD MOVEMENT**:
- ONLY if the path is a clearly centered open void, use `A1` to cross.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm (Use to break oscillation, or if centered and safe)
- `D1`: slide 5 cm left (to avoid right frame or correct rightward drift)
- `D2`: slide 5 cm right (to avoid left frame or correct leftward drift)
- `B2`, `C2`: rotate 45° (ONLY for Priority 1 Abort)

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES
- `Go Through Door`: Still crossing the door, correcting drift, or evading a frame.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Door is open but you lost alignment completely.
- `Position in the center of room`: Threshold crossed successfully (you are inside the new room).
- `Search open Door`: Path is blocked by a CLOSED object (ABORT).
