## State: **Go Through Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Proceed through the OPEN door. **CRITICAL: NEVER collide with the doorframes.** You should already be aligned at ~0° from the `Oriented Towards Door` state. Your job now is to advance straight through with minimal corrections.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Strict Priority)

🚫 **PRIORITY 0: VISIBILITY SAFETY CHECK (ALWAYS FIRST)**:
- If the image is **blurry, completely uniform, or you cannot identify any doorframe or open void at all**, you have lost visual reference.
- **DO NOT advance forward blindly.** Return to `Oriented Towards Door` state immediately — you need to re-find and re-align with the door.
- This rule overrides everything below, including anti-oscillation.

🔁 **PRIORITY 1: ANTI-OSCILLATION (FORCE ADVANCE)**:
- Inspect only the **last 4 commands** in `mov_history` (ignore older history — it may be from a previous state).
- If those last 4 commands contain **both `D1` and `D2`** (in any order), you are in an overcorrection loop.
- **CRITICAL**: Return `A1` immediately, regardless of how the frames look. Lateral corrections are making things worse.
- **DO NOT apply Priority 2 collision avoidance if oscillation is detected.**

🚨 **PRIORITY 2: CRITICAL EVASION ONLY (EXTREME COLLISION THRESHOLD)**:
- A collision is a FATAL ERROR, but you must **only react in genuinely extreme situations**.
- **"Extreme" means**: A single doorframe or wall occupies **more than 60% of the horizontal width** of the image, AND the open void is pushed to the far opposite edge. This is a genuine imminent collision.
- A frame that is "visible", "prominent", or takes up 20–50% of the view is **NORMAL** for a narrow doorway. **Do NOT trigger evasion for this.**
- **NARROW DOOR / BOTH FRAMES VISIBLE**: If you can see BOTH a left and a right doorframe with an open void between them — even if both feel close — YOU ARE CENTERED. **Do NOT slide.** Proceed with `A1`.
- **EQUALLY DISTANT FRAMES**: If both doorframes are arguably equidistant from the center of the image (the open void sits roughly in the middle), the robot is already centered. **Do NOT slide.** Return `A1` immediately.
- If and only if ONE frame exceeds the **60% threshold**:
  - If the **LEFT** frame is extremely close → slide RIGHT: `D2`.
  - If the **RIGHT** frame is extremely close → slide LEFT: `D1`.
- **After a single evasive slide, you MUST return to `A1` on the next step.** Do not chain lateral slides.
- Keep the state as `Go Through Door`. **CRITICAL: Do NOT rotate (`B1`/`B2`/`C1`/`C2`) inside the doorway.**

🚨 **PRIORITY 3: CRITICAL ABORT (ONLY CLOSED OBJECTS)**:
- ONLY abort if you face a **CLOSED DOOR, CABINET, or CLOSET** dead ahead.
- Switch state to `Search open Door` and rotate away (`B2` or `C2`).

✅ **PRIORITY 4: SAFE FORWARD MOVEMENT (DEFAULT ACTION)**:
- **This is the default.** As long as the open void is the central element in the image, return `A1`.
- **DO NOT use D1/D2 for "drift correction" or "fine adjustment" while inside the doorway.** The VLM misjudges proportions at close range, and lateral micro-adjustments cause fatal oscillations within the doorframe.
- **Rule: If you can see the open void ahead of you, advance with `A1`. Period.**
- Trust that the alignment from `Oriented Towards Door` is correct. Small visual asymmetries in the doorframes at close range are perspective distortions, not real misalignment.
- **GRAY ZONE SAFETY**: If a single doorframe occupies approximately **50% of the horizontal width** and the remaining open void appears narrow, pause and verify you are centered. If there is any doubt that advancing 10 cm could cause a collision (e.g., you are slightly angled toward that frame), treat this as an extreme case and apply Priority 2 evasion instead of advancing blindly.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm — **DEFAULT command. Use this unless extreme evasion is needed.**
- `D1`: slide 5 cm left — **ONLY for extreme evasion (>60% frame occupation). Never for drift correction.**
- `D2`: slide 5 cm right — **ONLY for extreme evasion (>60% frame occupation). Never for drift correction.**
- `B2`, `C2`: rotate 45° — **ONLY for Priority 3 Abort (closed object).**

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES
- `Go Through Door`: Still crossing the door or evading an extreme collision.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Door is open but you lost alignment completely (visibility lost).
- `Position in the center of room`: Threshold crossed successfully (you are inside the new room).
- `Search open Door`: Path is blocked by a CLOSED object (ABORT).
