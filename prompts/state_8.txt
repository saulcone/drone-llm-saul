## State: **Go Through Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
You are CLOSE to the door (>40% of frame). Now:
1. **CENTER** the door precisely using micro-rotations (B0/C0)
2. **CHECK LATERAL CLEARANCE** before advancing
3. **CROSS** when centered AND both sides clear

---

## ⚠️ CRITICAL: LATERAL WALL CHECK

**Before emitting A1 to advance, you MUST check both sides of the frame:**

| What you see | Meaning | Action |
|--------------|---------|--------|
| **LEFT side is mostly WALL** (gray texture >30% of frame) | Your body is too far LEFT | `D1` (slide RIGHT) |
| **RIGHT side is mostly WALL** (gray texture >30% of frame) | Your body is too far RIGHT | `D2` (slide LEFT) |
| **Both sides show doorframe edges symmetrically** | Safe to advance | `A1` |

**Example of UNSAFE advance**: Door opening appears centered, but the LEFT half of the image is a solid gray wall. If you advance (A1), you will HIT that left wall!

**Example of SAFE advance**: Door opening centered, and you can see small equal portions of doorframe on BOTH left and right edges.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Strict Evaluation Order)

🎯 **RULE 0: CENTER THE DOOR FIRST**:
- Use micro-rotations for precise alignment:
  | Door position | Action |
  |---------------|--------|
  | Slightly LEFT | `B0` (5° micro-rotation left) |
  | Clearly LEFT | `B1` (15° rotation left) |
  | Slightly RIGHT | `C0` (5° micro-rotation right) |
  | Clearly RIGHT | `C1` (15° rotation right) |
  | CENTERED | Go to RULE 0b |

📐 **RULE 0b: LATERAL CLEARANCE CHECK (MANDATORY before A1)**:
- **Divide the frame in half vertically.**
- **LEFT HALF**: Is it mostly wall/solid texture? Or can you see through to the next room?
- **RIGHT HALF**: Is it mostly wall/solid texture? Or can you see through to the next room?
- **Decision**:
  - LEFT half is wall, RIGHT half is open → `D1` (slide right)
  - RIGHT half is wall, LEFT half is open → `D2` (slide left)  
  - BOTH halves show opening/symmetric → `A1` (safe to advance)

🛑 **RULE 1: VISIBILITY SAFETY CHECK**:
- **Condition**: Image is blurry, uniform, or no doorframe visible.
- **Action**: Rotate away (`B2` or `C2`) and switch to `Search open Door`.

🔁 **RULE 2: ANTI-OSCILLATION**:
- **Condition**: Last 4 commands contain both `D1` and `D2`.
- **Action**: Return `A1` immediately to break the loop.

✅ **RULE 3: CROSS WHEN CLEAR**:
- **Condition**: Door CENTERED + both sides show symmetric clearance.
- **Action**: `A1` to advance through.
- **Keep emitting A1** until door frame is BEHIND you.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

| Command | When to use |
|---------|-------------|
| `A1` | Door CENTERED + no lateral wall close — advance to cross |
| `B0` | Door slightly LEFT — micro-rotate 5° left |
| `B1` | Door clearly LEFT — rotate 15° left |
| `C0` | Door slightly RIGHT — micro-rotate 5° right |
| `C1` | Door clearly RIGHT — rotate 15° right |
| `D1` | Wall/frame close on LEFT — slide right to clear it |
| `D2` | Wall/frame close on RIGHT — slide left to clear it |
| `B2`/`C2` | Abort/clipping recovery only |

---

## ⚠️ DECISION FLOW

```
1. Is door CENTERED in frame?
   - NO, LEFT  → B0 or B1 (rotate to center)
   - NO, RIGHT → C0 or C1 (rotate to center)
   - YES → Go to step 2

2. Door is centered. CHECK LATERAL CLEARANCE:
   - Look at LEFT HALF of image: Is it mostly WALL?
   - Look at RIGHT HALF of image: Is it mostly WALL?
   
   - LEFT is wall, RIGHT is open → D1 (slide right)
   - RIGHT is wall, LEFT is open → D2 (slide left)
   - BOTH sides symmetric/clear  → A1 (safe to cross)

3. After A1, check if door frame is behind you:
   - Frame still visible ahead → Loop back to step 1
   - Frame behind → "Position in the center of room"
```

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Go Through Door`: Still centering or crossing.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Lost alignment completely.
- `Position in the center of room`: Door frame is BEHIND you — you have crossed.
- `Search open Door`: Path blocked or lost door.
