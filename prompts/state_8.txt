## State: **Go Through Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Proceed through the centered OPEN door confidently.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES

🚨 **CRITICAL ABORT (DO NOT HIT CABINETS)**:
- If the object in front of you has knobs, handles, or is explicitly a **CLOSED DOOR, CABINET, or CLOSET**:
- **YOU MUST ABORT**. Switch state to `Search open Door`.
- Return movement `E` or `B2` to abort. 
- **IT IS STRICTLY FORBIDDEN TO USE `A1` IF THE DOOR IS CLOSED.**

- **Forward Movement**:
- Use `A1` **only** if the door is clearly an **open void**, fully visible, and dominant in the center.

- **Refine Alignment**:
- If the open door becomes uncentered, switch to `Oriented Towards Door`.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm (ONLY IF OPEN VOID)
- `E`, `B1`, `B2`, `C1`, `C2`: Use to abort if closed.

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES
- `Go Through Door`: Door is open, centered, and safe to cross.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Door is open but off-center.
- `Position in the center of room`: Threshold crossed.
- `Search open Door`: The door is CLOSED or is a cabinet (ABORT).
