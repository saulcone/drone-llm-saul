## State: **Go Through Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Proceed through the centered OPEN door confidently without hugging the doorframes.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES

🚨 **CRITICAL ABORT (BLINDNESS & CABINETS)**:
- If your view is suddenly completely blocked by a surface (you are glued to a wall/doorframe), OR if you face a **CLOSED DOOR, CABINET, or CLOSET**:
  - **YOU MUST ABORT**. Switch state to `Search open Door`.
  - Return `B2` or `C2` to rotate away and regain field of view.
  - **IT IS STRICTLY FORBIDDEN TO USE `A1` IF YOUR VISION IS BLOCKED.**

- **Drift Correction (lateral)**:
  - If you are moving through the door but drifting towards one side frame, slide away: `D1` (drift right → slide left) or `D2` (drift left → slide right).
  - Do NOT rotate to correct drift — you would lose the door in your view.

- **Forward Movement**:
  - Use `A1` **only** if the door is clearly an **open void**, fully visible, and you can see the room ahead.

- **Refine Alignment**:
  - If the open door becomes very uncentered (more than half the frame), switch to `Oriented Towards Door` to recenter before proceeding.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm (ONLY IF PATH IS CLEAR AND OPEN)
- `D1`: slide 10 cm left (to correct rightward drift while keeping camera on door)
- `D2`: slide 10 cm right (to correct leftward drift while keeping camera on door)
- `B2`, `C2`: rotate 45° — use ONLY to abort when path is blocked

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES
- `Go Through Door`: Door is open, centered, and safe to cross.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Door is open but you drifted off-center.
- `Position in the center of room`: Threshold crossed successfully.
- `Search open Door`: The path is BLOCKED, closed, or you are completely glued to a frame (ABORT).
