## State: **Search open Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Systematically scan the environment to detect an open door that may lead to another room.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES

- Use small in-place rotations and short forward steps to explore the space.
- Prioritize rotation if the robot has not moved recently or the field of view hasn’t changed.
- If the previous movement was a rotation, continue rotating in the same direction for consistency and full panoramic coverage.
  - Example: if `previous_movement` was `B2`, prefer `B1`, `B2`, or `B3`.
- If a door-like structure is detected but partially occluded or far from the center, rotate slightly toward it.
- Use forward movement (`A1`) when:
  - The current view is fully scanned and no door is detected,
  - Or the scene appears cluttered and needs positional adjustment.
- Do not switch to another state unless a door is clearly visible.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return **ONE** movement command from the list below:

### A. Forward movement:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm

### B. Left rotation:
- `B1`: rotate 15°
- `B2`: rotate 45°
- `B3`: rotate 90°

### C. Right rotation:
- `C1`: rotate 15°
- `C2`: rotate 45°
- `C3`: rotate 90°

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

Select the next FSM state based on visibility of the door:

- `Search open Door`: if no open door is currently visible or recognizable.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: if an open door is detected and approximately centered, or reachable by slight rotation.

---

## ⚠️ NOTES

- Do not advance through the door in this state — only search and identify.
- Avoid unnecessary forward motion unless the view is obstructed or very repetitive.
- Return only a single valid movement command and state.
