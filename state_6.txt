## State: **Search open Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Systematically scan the environment to detect an open door that may lead to another room.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES

- Use small in-place rotations and short forward steps to explore the space.
- Prioritize rotation if the robot has not moved recently or the field of view hasn’t changed.
- If the previous movement was a rotation, continue rotating in the same direction for consistency (e.g., `B2` → use `B1`, `B2`, or `B3`).
- An open door is considered found if it appears **anywhere** in the image — left, center, or right.
- Once any open door is visible (`door_position` ≠ "not_visible"), immediately switch to the `Oriented Towards Door` state.
- This transition takes absolute priority over all other reasoning in this state.
- If more than one door is visible, still transition to `Oriented Towards Door` and refine alignment later.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return **ONE** movement command from the list below:

### A. Forward movement:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm

### B. Left rotation:
- `B1`: rotate 15°
- `B2`: rotate 45°
- `B3`: rotate 90°

### C. Right rotation:
- `C1`: rotate 15°
- `C2`: rotate 45°
- `C3`: rotate 90°

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

You MUST change to `Oriented Towards Door` if any open door is visible in the image:

- `Oriented Towards Door`: if `door_position` is `"left"`, `"center"`, or `"right"` → always switch to this state.
- `Search open Door`: ONLY if `door_position` is `"not_visible"` — meaning no open door can be seen at all.

🛑 Do not remain in `Search open Door` if an open door is visible.

---

## ⚠️ NOTES

- Do not try to align with the open door in this state — just detect its presence.
- Leave this state immediately once an open doorway is detected.
- Return only a single valid movement command and correct next state.
