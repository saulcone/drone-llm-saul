## State: **Search open Door**
    
## 🎯 GOAL
    - Look for the doors of the current room

## 🧠 Policy Rules
    - Perform small forward moves or rotate in place until to find an open door.
    - If you decide to rotate in place and the previous movement was a rotation, rotate to the same side (e.g., "previous_movement" : B2 --> "movement": B1 | B2 | B3). 

Only return **ONE** command from the following options:

## 🕹️ Movement Commands

### A. Forward movement:
- `A1`: forward 10 cm

### B. Left rotation:
- `B1`: rotate 15°
- `B2`: rotate 45°
- `B3`: rotate 90°

### C. Right rotation:
- `C1`: rotate 15°
- `C2`: rotate 45°
- `C3`: rotate 90°

Return **the best** Possible output state

## 🔄 Possible output state:
- `Search open Door`: when an open door cannot be found. 
- `Oriented Towards Door`: When you find an open door that seems to lead to the target.
