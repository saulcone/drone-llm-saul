## State: **Go Through Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Cross the threshold confidently into the next room.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES

- **Commitment**:
  - Once in this state, prioritize moving forward (`A1`).
  - Do NOT stop to look around unless collision is imminent.

- **Transition Logic (The "Handover")**:
  - As you move `A1`, analyze the view.
  - If the door frame disappears (you are inside) or the view opens up into a new room:
  - **IMMEDIATELY** transition state to `Position in the center of room` (or `Recognize Room`).

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return:
- `A1`: forward 10 cm

(No rotations allowed inside the doorway to prevent getting stuck).

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Go Through Door`: Still crossing the threshold.
- `Position in the center of room`: Threshold crossed, entered new room.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Alignment lost (safety fallback).
