## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Align the robot directly in front of the target open door and approach.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Strict Priority)

1️⃣ **FORWARD PRIORITY (Avoid Oscillation)**:
   - If `door_position` is "center": **ALWAYS move `A1`**. Never rotate.
   - If `door_position` is "left" or "right" but the door is **huge/close**: Move `A1` (entering obliquely is better than over-correcting).

2️⃣ **ROTATION CORRECTION**:
   - If door is "left" (and far): Use `B1`.
   - If door is "right" (and far): Use `C1`.

3️⃣ **LATERAL CORRECTION**:
   - Use `D1`/`D2` ONLY if the door is "center" but you are physically offset (parallel shift), and you are close.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return **ONE** movement command:

### A. Forward:
- `A1`: 10 cm

### B/C. Rotation:
- `B1`: Left 15° | `C1`: Right 15°

### D. Lateral:
- `D1`: Left 10 cm | `D2`: Right 10 cm

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Go Through Door`: Door is centered and very close (fills view).
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Door visible but needs approach/alignment.
- `Search open Door`: Door lost from view.
