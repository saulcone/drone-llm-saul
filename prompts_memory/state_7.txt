## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Align the robot directly in front of the target open door and approach.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Strict Priority)

1️⃣ **ROTATION PRIORITY (Center the Target First)**:
   - If `door_position` is "left": **You MUST use `B1`**. Do NOT move forward.
   - If `door_position` is "right": **You MUST use `C1`**. Do NOT move forward.
   - **Reasoning**: Moving forward while the door is on the side causes the robot to miss the entrance. Align first.

2️⃣ **LATERAL CORRECTION (Fine Tuning)**:
   - If `door_position` is "center" but you are physically offset (e.g. seeing the frame side):
   - Use `D1` (Left) or `D2` (Right) to position perfectly in front of the void.

3️⃣ **FORWARD APPROACH**:
   - Only use `A1` if `door_position` is **"center"**.
   - If the door is centered and looks open/void, advance confidently.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return **ONE** movement command:

### A. Forward:
- `A1`: 10 cm

### B/C. Rotation:
- `B1`: Left 15° | `C1`: Right 15°

### D. Lateral:
- `D1`: Left 10 cm | `D2`: Right 10 cm

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Go Through Door`: Door is centered, fills >50% of image, and appears OPEN.
- `Oriented Towards Door`: Door visible but needs approach/alignment.
- `Search open Door`: Door lost from view.
