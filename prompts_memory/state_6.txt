## State: **Search open Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Find an open door that leads to the goal room based on the `topological_map`.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (Priority Ordered)

1️⃣ **Unknown Room Beyond Door**:
   - If you see an open door (`door_position` != "not_visible") but can't identify the room behind it:
   - MUST move forward (`A1`) to see inside. Do NOT rotate away.

2️⃣ **Path Validation (Topological Reasoning)**:
   - If you identify the room beyond the door:
   - Check `topological_map`: Is this room the goal? OR does it connect to the goal?
   - **YES**: Switch to `Oriented Towards Door` (return `movement: "E"`).
   - **NO**: Ignore this door and continue searching (rotate/explore).

3️⃣ **Search Strategy**:
   - If no door is visible, explore using rotations (`B2`, `C2`) and short moves (`A1`).

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return **ONE** movement command:

### A. Forward:
- `A1`: 10 cm

### B/C. Rotation:
- `B1`, `B2`, `B3` | `C1`, `C2`, `C3`

### E. Transition:
- `E`: ONLY used to signal "Door Found and Validated" -> Switch state.

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Oriented Towards Door`: Open door found AND validates against map (leads to goal).
- `Search open Door`: No door seen, or door leads to wrong room.
