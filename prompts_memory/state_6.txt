## State: **Search open Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Systematically explore the environment to detect an open door that may lead to the **goal room**.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return **ONE** movement command from the list below:

### A. Forward movement:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm

### B. Left rotation:
- `B1`: rotate 15°  
- `B2`: rotate 45°  
- `B3`: rotate 90°

### C. Right rotation:
- `C1`: rotate 15°  
- `C2`: rotate 45°  
- `C3`: rotate 90°

### E. Transition:
- `E`: issue exclusively when switching to `Oriented Towards Door`.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES (priority ordered)

1️⃣ **VISUAL CLUE & SAFETY OVERRIDE (CRITICAL)** If an open door is visible (`door_position` is "left", "center", or "right") AND you see **ANY visual clue** related to the goal room (e.g., specific floor texture, tiles, sink, towel, bed):
- **IMMEDIATELY** switch to `Oriented Towards Door` (return `movement: "E"`).
- **Reasoning**: It is safer to align first than to move parallel to the wall (`A1`) which causes collisions.
- **DO NOT** wait for 100% room identification. If it *looks like* the target, align now.

2️⃣ **Path Validation (Topological Reasoning)** If you confirm the room identity beyond the door:
- Check `topological_map`:
  - **Valid Path?** (Target room OR connected to target) -> Switch to `Oriented Towards Door` (`movement: "E"`).
  - **Invalid Path?** -> Ignore this door and continue searching (rotate).

3️⃣ **Unknown Room Strategy** If an open door is visible but completely unknown/dark:
- If `door_position` is **"center"**: Move `A1` to see inside.
- If `door_position` is **"left"** or **"right"**: Switch to `Oriented Towards Door` (`movement: "E"`) to center it. **Do NOT move `A1` sideways.**

4️⃣ **General Search** If no door is visible (`door_position = "not_visible"`):
- Rotate (`B2`, `C2`) to scan the area.

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Oriented Towards Door`: 
  - Door found with visual clues of target room.
  - OR Door found and leads to target (topological check).
  - OR Door visible but needs centering (too far to side).
  - **Action**: Must return `movement: "E"`.

- `Search open Door`: 
  - No door visible.
  - Visible door leads to wrong room.

---

## ⚠️ NOTES

- **Collision Prevention**: Never move `A1` if the door is on the edge of the screen (`left`/`right`) and you are close. Switch state to align.
- Prioritize visual clues (colors, objects) over full confidence.
