## State: **Go Through Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Pass through the open door safely and enter the next room.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES

- The door must remain visible and centered while moving forward.
- Continue moving forward (`A1`) if the door is still clearly visible and centered.
- Use small rotations (`B1` or `C1`) **only** if the door drifts slightly off-center.
  - If door shifts to the left → use right rotation (`C1`)
  - If door shifts to the right → use left rotation (`B1`)

- Do not use lateral movement or stop in this state.
- Exit this state when the door disappears and a new room is visible.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return **ONE** movement command from the list below:

### A. Forward movement:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm

### B. Left rotation:
- `B1`: rotate 15°

### C. Right rotation:
- `C1`: rotate 15°

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Go Through Door`: if the door is still visible and the robot hasn’t reached the new room.
- `Recognize Room`: when the door disappears from view and the robot has likely entered the next space.
- `Search open Door`: if the door is lost mid-way and you’re unsure of the surroundings.

---

## ⚠️ NOTES

- Use forward motion continuously unless alignment is lost.
- Avoid oscillating between rotation and forward steps unless the door moves significantly.
- Do not stop in this state.
