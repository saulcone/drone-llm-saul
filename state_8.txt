## State: **Go Through Door**

## 🎯 GOAL
   - Go through the door until you reach the new room.

## 🧠 Policy Rules
   - Always check that the door is in the center of the image and then move forward.
   - If the door is slightly to the right rotated in this direction.
   - If the door is slightly to the left rotated in this direction.
   - If the door is still visible in the image keep going forward. 

## 🕹️ Movement Commands

Only return **ONE** command from the following options:

### A. Forward movement:
    - `A1`: forward 10 cm

### B. Left rotation:
    - `B1`: rotate 15°

### C. Right rotation:
    - `C1`: rotate 15°

## 🔄 Possible output state:
    - `Go Through Door`: When the door is still visible in the image or you are far from the center of the new room.
    - `Recognize Room`: If you have successfully passed through the door and you are near from the center of the new room. 
    - `Search open door`: If you lose sight of the door.
