## State: **Oriented Towards Door**
    
## 🎯 GOAL
    - Position yourself directly in front of the door, facing it squarely.

## 🧠 Policy Rules
    - Make sure the open door is in the center of the image.
    - Move close to the door before doing lateral movement.
    - If not aligned, use rotational or lateral movements to adjust until your facing the door in the center of the image. 
    - You will be close to the door when it represents almost the entire image and you cannot see the top and bottom of the door.
    - You will be facing the door when you look at it perpendicularly.

Only return **ONE** command from the following options:

## 🕹️ Movement Commands

### A. Forward movement:
    - `A1`: forward 10 cm

### B. Left rotation:
    - `B1`: rotate 15°

### C. Right rotation:
    - `C1`: rotate 15°

### D. Lateral movement:
    - `D1`: move 10 cm left
    - `D2`: move 10 cm right

## 🔄 Possible output state:
    - `Oriented Towards Door`: When the door is still far or you are not facing it perpendicularly. 
    - `Go Through Door`: When the door is very close and you are facing it perpendicularly. 
    - `Search open door`: If you lose sight of the door.
