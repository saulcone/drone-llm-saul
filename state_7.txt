## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Align the robot directly in front of an open door, centered and facing it perpendicularly.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES

- The door must be centered horizontally in the image and appear **large** (close to the robot).
- You are considered well-aligned when:
  - The door occupies most of the image width.
  - The top and bottom of the door are not visible (i.e., it’s tall and close).
  - It appears perpendicular to the viewing angle (flat front-on view).

- Use small rotational or lateral movements to adjust alignment:
  - Use `B1`, `C1` for fine rotation.
  - Use `D1` or `D2` if the door is off-center but close.

- Use forward movement (`A1`) **only** when already aligned and needing minor positional adjustment.

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return **ONE** movement command from the list below:

### A. Forward movement:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm

### B. Left rotation:
- `B1`: rotate 15°

### C. Right rotation:
- `C1`: rotate 15°

### D. Lateral movement:
- `D1`: move 10 cm left  
- `D2`: move 10 cm right

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

- `Oriented Towards Door`: if the door is still misaligned, far, or not yet centered.
- `Go Through Door`: when the door is centered, large, and you are aligned to cross.
- `Search open Door`: if the door is lost from view.

---

## ⚠️ NOTES

- Avoid moving forward prematurely.
- Favor rotation/lateral movement over forward if the door is not yet properly aligned.
