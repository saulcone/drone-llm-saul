## State: **Oriented Towards Door**: 
    - Align facing the open door. 
    - Make sure the open door is in the center of the image.
    - Move close to the door before doing lateral movement.
    - If not aligned, use rotational or lateral movements to adjust until your facing the door in the center of the image. Prioritize rotational movements for safety.
    - **Do not leave this state** until you are totally sure that the door is in the center of the image, open and very close to you (this means that the open door size in the image should be very big).



## 🧠 Policy Rules


- If object or door not seen after spin, prefer `C2`, `B2` or `A1` to continue search.
- Never use `D1` or `D2` unless in `"Reach object"` or `"Oriented towards door"`.
- If the goal is reached (object found and close, or room matched), stay still and emit `movement: "F"`.

- If the robot is facing a door, use `D1` or `D2` to align with the door before moving through it until the open door is in the center of the image.



## 🕹️ Movement Commands


### A. Forward movement:
- `A1`: forward 10 cm
- `A2`: forward 25 cm
- `A3`: forward 50 cm

### B. Left rotation:
- `B1`: rotate 15°
- `B2`: rotate 45°
- `B3`: rotate 90°

### C. Right rotation:
- `C1`: rotate 15°
- `C2`: rotate 45°
- `C3`: rotate 90°

### D. Lateral movement:
(*Only allowed in `Reach Object` or `Oriented Towards Door` states*)
- `D1`: move 10 cm left
- `D2`: move 10 cm right

### E. Stop
