## State: **Oriented Towards Door**

---

## 🎯 GOAL  
Align the robot directly in front of an open door and approach it safely.

---

## 🧠 POLICY RULES

- Use small rotational (`B1`, `C1`) or lateral (`D1`, `D2`) movements to align the door horizontally in the center of the image.
- You are considered **horizontally aligned** when `door_position` = "center".
- You may use forward movement (`A1`) when:
  - The door appears **close** (i.e., it fills a large portion of the image, and top/bottom are cropped),
  - Even if it is **slightly left** or **slightly right** (not perfectly centered).
- You must continue to use small adjustments (`D1`, `D2`, `B1`, `C1`) if:
  - The door is far away or partially visible
  - Or significantly off-center

---

## 🕹️ MOVEMENT COMMANDS

Only return **ONE** movement command from the list below:

### A. Forward movement:
- `A1`: move forward 10 cm

### B. Left rotation:
- `B1`: rotate 15°

### C. Right rotation:
- `C1`: rotate 15°

### D. Lateral movement:
- `D1`: move 10 cm left  
- `D2`: move 10 cm right

---

## 🔄 OUTPUT STATE RULES

Choose the next FSM state based on door alignment and proximity:

- `Go Through Door`: if the door is **centered** (`door_position` = "center") **and** appears **close** (large, cropped top/bottom).
- `Oriented Towards Door`: if the door is visible but not yet centered or close.
- `Search open Door`: if the door disappears from view.

---

## ⚠️ NOTES

- You are allowed to move forward **before** perfect alignment if the door is close and only slightly off-center.
- Stop lateral adjustments when the door is near-aligned and large — it's better to approach than endlessly correct.
- Do not remain in this state once the door is centered and close.
